# Continuity Rule

- Add items only when actually acquired or established.
- Remove or reduce items only when sold, lost, used, consumed, destroyed, transferred, spent, or otherwise legitimately changed.
- Preserve quantities, equipped/stored state, charges, durability, attunement, special effects, curses, identified/unknown properties, and location when relevant.
- For current party NPCs, `MOCK_NPC-state.md` owns the master ownership list and `MOCK_inventory.md` owns expanded active mechanical bookkeeping.
- During an unfinished turn, `MOCK_turn_save.md` temporarily overlays quantities, charges, ammunition, resources, and item state until end-turn reconciliation.
