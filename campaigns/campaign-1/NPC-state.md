## Ownership Rule

`NPC-state.md` owns persistent NPC identity, statistics, condition, relationships, and party membership.

- `inventory.md` owns tracked items and resources carried by NPCs.
- `world_state.md` may reference NPCs when they matter to locations, factions, quests, clues, or world consequences, but should not duplicate their full NPC record.
- `session_log.md` records chronological events involving NPCs without replacing their current persistent state here.

## Fresh-Start Rule

Do not recover or reconstruct NPC canon from deleted files, repository history, previous chats, memory, or another campaign unless the player explicitly requests a specific import.
