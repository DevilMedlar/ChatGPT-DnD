## Checkpoints

### Session 1, Turn 1 — Brasshaven Opening Investigation

**Starting location:** Brasshaven — Wayfarer Gate.

DevilMedlar privately told Senpai, "you are so beautiful, so what do you want to do? explore, look at the flyer, question the merchant about these stones, or the woman?" He gave Senpai control of her own preference and stated that if she chose anything except exploring first, he would question the merchant, then the silver-haired woman, then check the flyer, then explore Brasshaven with Senpai before deciding what to do next.

Senpai received the compliment with amused appreciation and chose to investigate the relic stones first, reasoning that the stones were actively being repacked and might disappear into storage while the flyer would remain posted and the silver-haired woman appeared to want to be noticed.

#### Merchant and relic stones

DevilMedlar and Senpai questioned **Orin Pell**, human male age 46, a Brasshaven stone-and-antiquities broker whose wagon axle had broken at the gate.

Orin stated that the carved stones came from **Cinderwash Cut**, a salvage excavation approximately two days east along Emberglass Road. The supplier is **Gravelark Salvage**, which has been removing carved stone from a collapsed roadside shrine beneath dark volcanic glass. Orin purchased six crates but received only four. He was told the workers uncovered a sealed lower stair or chamber and refused to continue deeper work.

The stones include a recurring emblem showing a dragon curled around a stylized sun. It is not Clan Aurivane's crest, although its radial geometry feels uncomfortably familiar to DevilMedlar.

- **DevilMedlar History:** d20 = 1, History +1, total **2**. Failure. DevilMedlar could not reliably identify the emblem's culture, age, or relationship to Clan Aurivane. No false historical information was given.
- **Senpai Arcana:** d20 = 7, Arcana +2, total **9**. Failure. Senpai could not identify an active magical function or confidently determine the nature of any ancient magical residue in the stone.

#### Silver-haired woman

DevilMedlar next approached the silver-haired woman from the tea stall and directly questioned why she had been watching his signet.

She introduced herself as **Seraphine Vale**, half-elf female age 34, a traveling antiquarian and researcher. Seraphine said she recognized the Aurivane Dawnscale Signet from historical seal research and had spent years cataloguing dragon-lineage seals and related eastern ruins. She did not claim the Cinderwash emblem was Aurivane, but said its symbolic structure resembles conventions found in old eastern dragon-road shrines.

Seraphine also indicated that she believes the Cinderwash relic traffic and the Emberglass caravan disappearances may be related enough to deserve caution. She said she can be found through the **Three Lanterns Inn** if DevilMedlar and Senpai want to speak again.

- **DevilMedlar Insight:** d20 = 18, Insight +4, total **22**. Strong success. DevilMedlar judged Seraphine truthful about recognizing the signet and about her concern over the relic/caravan connection. He also recognized that she was intentionally withholding who finances her current research and some details of why she is in Brasshaven.

No alliance, debt, trust bond, attraction, hostility, or quest agreement with Seraphine was established.

#### Missing-caravan flyer

DevilMedlar and Senpai then read the Merchants' Compact notice in full. It offers a **60 gp base reward** for locating three caravans that disappeared along Emberglass Road during the last twelve days and returning reliable proof of their fate. The most recent disappearance occurred three days before Session 1 began. Rescue, recovery, or exceptional-hazard compensation may be negotiated separately if circumstances justify it. The contract is handled through **Merchants' Compact Hall**.

DevilMedlar and Senpai learned the lead but did **not** formally accept the contract.

#### Exploring Brasshaven

The pair then explored Brasshaven together before choosing their next direction.

- **Senpai Perception:** d20 = 19, Perception +3, total **22**. Strong success.

Senpai quickly learned the useful layout around **Lantern Square**, Brasshaven's central public square. The pair identified the **Three Lanterns Inn**, **Merchants' Compact Hall**, **Brass Nail Smithy**, **Mooncup Apothecary**, and **Steam & Stone Baths**. Senpai also detected no one consistently following them during the walk.

**Ending location:** Brasshaven — Lantern Square.

**Resources:** No HP, spell slots, Dragon Ki, Radiant Breath uses, Veiled Form uses, Veilstep uses, consumables, or currency were spent.

**XP award:** DevilMedlar gains **50 XP** and Senpai gains **50 XP** for meaningful investigation, connecting multiple leads, and useful exploration. Both are now at **50 / 300 XP** toward level 2.

**Turn result:** The opening investigation produced three unaccepted leads: the Cinderwash relic site, the Emberglass missing-caravan contract, and Seraphine Vale's partially withheld research. DevilMedlar retains full choice over what to pursue next.
