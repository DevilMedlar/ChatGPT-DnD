# Reproductive Species Complete Profiles — Batch 17

## Purpose

This batch resolves plant, fungal, ooze, transformed, and metamorphic species where biological appearance alone is especially unreliable as a guide to reproduction.

The current gate still requires stable biologically male and female reproductive roles. A lifecycle may be entirely biological and still fail if it is asexual, spore-transfer only, dual-role, or otherwise outside that restricted system.

# Volodni

- **Species:** Volodni / Pine Folk
- **Source / Version:** Forgotten Realms, `Unapproachable East` and compatible Volodni lore
- **Creature Type:** Plant
- **Profile Status:** Complete
- **Evidence Basis:** Source establishes distinct male and female Volodni, stable generations, adults who were born as Volodni, parents, youths, and families; reproductive anatomy uses a homebrew descendant-lineage baseline
- **Compatibility Family:** Internal Live-Bearing Humanoid / Plant-Humanoid Lineage
- **Reproductive Mode:** Sexual
- **Male Reproductive Role:** supplies the fertilizing biological contribution
- **Female Reproductive Role:** supplies the ovum-equivalent contribution and carries the developing offspring
- **Fertilization Method:** Internal
- **Development Method:** Live-bearing
- **Carrier / Host:** Female parent
- **Special Biological Requirements:** functioning compatible internal-fertilization anatomy and sufficient nutrition/water for plant-humanoid development
- **Birth / Hatching Constraints:** barklike skin, woody tissues, sap-bearing physiology, and other rigid plant traits remain sufficiently soft and neonatal-safe at birth and mature afterward
- **General Crossbreeding Potential:** Cross-species compatible
- **Known Naturally Compatible:** Volodni + Volodni
- **Known Cross-Species Compatibility:** completed internal-fertilization species when anatomy is workable and no specific incompatibility exists
- **Known Incompatibilities:** asexual/spore-only plant peoples do not become compatible merely because Volodni are Plant creatures
- **Notes:** The first Volodni were Humans transformed by a sylvan power, but later Volodni are a stable born population. Creature Type Plant does not make their current reproduction asexual or magical.

# Opteran

- **Species:** Opteran
- **Source / Version:** `Heliana's Guide to Monster Hunting`
- **Creature Type:** Humanoid, also Fey for applicable effects in the source
- **Profile Status:** Complete
- **Evidence Basis:** Source explicitly establishes larval and reproductive adult stages, adults finding mates and producing new batches of larvae; stable male/female sex roles and egg fertilization use the repository's lepidopteran biological-family inference
- **Compatibility Family:** Internal Egg-Laying Insectoid / Lepidopteran
- **Reproductive Mode:** Sexual
- **Male Reproductive Role:** adult male supplies the fertilizing biological contribution
- **Female Reproductive Role:** adult female supplies the ova, forms fertilized eggs, and lays the reproductive clutch under the lepidopteran family baseline
- **Fertilization Method:** Internal
- **Development Method:** Egg -> larva -> chrysalis -> winged reproductive adult
- **Carrier / Host:** Female parent until egg-laying; eggs/larvae develop externally afterward
- **Special Biological Requirements:** only the winged adult life stage is reproductively mature; larval Opterans do not reproduce
- **Birth / Hatching Constraints:** eggs hatch or develop into small larval young; wings and full adult body size appear only after later metamorphosis
- **General Crossbreeding Potential:** Cross-species compatible with other completed internal-fertilization species when anatomy, egg viability, and developmental biology are workable
- **Known Naturally Compatible:** Opteran + Opteran
- **Known Conditional Pairings:** cross-species offspring must support an Opteran-style larval/metamorphic route when an Opteran female carries/forms the eggs, unless a specific bridge establishes another viable expression
- **Notes:** Opteran metamorphosis is an irreversible biological life-stage transition, not free shapechanging and not a reproductive workaround. It therefore does not violate the current no-shapechanger rule.

# Resolved exclusions

## Heliana Oozekin — Not a breeder under current global rules

- **Species:** Oozekin
- **Source / Version:** `Heliana's Guide to Monster Hunting`
- **Status:** Ineligible under the current global breeding scope
- **Source-Established Reproduction:** self-replication through asexual reproduction; an Oozekin produces larval sludge that develops into a new Oozekin after feeding and metamorphosis
- **Failed Gate:** stable male/female sexual reproduction
- **Notes:** The lifecycle is biological but asexual. Biological does not equal eligible under the current restricted gate.

## Mycelian — Not a breeder under current global rules

- **Species:** Mycelian
- **Source / Version:** `Heliana's Guide to Monster Hunting`
- **Status:** Ineligible under the current global breeding scope
- **Source-Established Lifecycle:** Mycelial spores inoculate a corpse, fungal tissues replace the host's organs, and the Mycelian can later redeploy its own spores into another corpse
- **Failed Gate:** stable male/female sexual reproduction and ordinary two-parent offspring development
- **Notes:** Mycelians use an entirely biological fungal lifecycle, but it is a spore/host propagation mechanism rather than the currently allowed male/female sexual system.

# Meaningful Holds

## Cnidaran — Hold under stable male/female-role rule

- **Species:** Cnidaran
- **Source / Version:** `Heliana's Guide to Monster Hunting`
- **Known Reproduction:** source explicitly establishes mass mating among mature medusozoa, conception of thousands of larvae, settlement into polyps, and each polyp growing/birthing a new medusozoa
- **Development Method:** sexual mating -> larvae -> sessile polyp -> medusozoa
- **Unresolved Gate Requirement:** the source does not establish stable biologically male and female reproductive roles. The communal mating process can involve dozens of individuals and parentage is effectively untracked.
- **Profile Status:** Hold under the current restricted global scope
- **Why This Is Not Ineligible Forever:** Cnidaran reproduction is clearly sexual and biologically detailed. The only current blocker is the repository's stable male/female-role requirement.
- **Future Override:** if the player later permits multi-role, hermaphroditic, broadcast, or otherwise non-binary biological sex systems, Cnidaran should be promoted immediately with a dedicated External/Communal Aquatic lifecycle profile.

## Ombrask — Hold as transformed adult state

- **Species / State:** Ombrask
- **Source / Version:** `Heliana's Guide to Monster Hunting`
- **Known Origin:** individuals of many existing species become Ombrasks after prolonged exposure to the Shadow Plane; they retain the general form and lifespan of their former species
- **Unresolved Reproduction:** source does not establish whether transformed Ombrasks retain functional reproductive anatomy, whether they can conceive as Ombrasks, or whether any child can be born as an Ombrask
- **Profile Status:** Hold
- **Notes:** The former species' reproductive profile cannot be silently copied after a body-altering Shadow Plane transformation. If a campaign establishes that an Ombrask retains reproductive function, use the former species as the biological base and record the Ombrask changes separately.

## Biological metamorphosis versus prohibited shapechanging

The current no-shapechanger rule does **not** prohibit ordinary biological life-stage development.

Allowed examples can include:

- egg -> larva -> adult
- tadpole -> adult
- caterpillar-like larva -> chrysalis -> winged adult
- juvenile aquatic form -> terrestrial adult

when those are fixed one-way or normal developmental stages of the species.

The exclusion targets species that freely assume alternate bodies/forms, require a temporary transformed body to reproduce, or otherwise change reproductive form in the prohibited shapechanger sense.

Opteran is the current explicit example of allowed biological metamorphosis.
