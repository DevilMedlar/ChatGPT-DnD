# Reproductive Species Complete Profiles — Batch 4

## Purpose

This batch establishes reproductive profiles for aquatic and amphibious peoples whose fertilization methods differ enough that appearance alone cannot decide compatibility.

It especially distinguishes:

- internal fertilization with aquatic egg-laying
- external spawning/fertilization
- internal live-bearing aquatic humanoids established as repository homebrew baselines where source lore is silent
- source-specific incompatibilities

All cross-species interaction uses `REPRODUCTIVE_FERTILIZATION_COMPATIBILITY.md`.

# Sahuagin

## Sahuagin — General

- **Species:** Sahuagin
- **Source / Version:** Forgotten Realms / AD&D `The Sea Devils` biological lineage carried forward as homebrew-compatible D&D lore
- **Creature Type:** Humanoid in modern D&D; older editions vary
- **Profile Status:** Complete
- **Evidence Basis:** Source material explicitly describes male/female sahuagin, mating, internally fertilized eggs, egg laying, incubation, hatchlings, and breeding age
- **Compatibility Family:** Internal Egg-Laying Aquatic
- **Reproductive Mode:** Sexual
- **Male Reproductive Role:** supplies the fertilizing biological contribution
- **Female Reproductive Role:** supplies ova, receives internal fertilization, forms eggs, and lays them
- **Fertilization Method:** Internal
- **Development Method:** Egg-laying
- **Carrier / Host:** Female until laying; eggs then develop in a communal or protected aquatic incubator/hatchery
- **Special Biological Requirements:** viable internal fertilization plus an aquatic incubation environment suitable for sahuagin eggs
- **Birth / Hatching Constraints:** hatchlings begin small and aquatic; adult fins, size, additional arms when inherited, and other mature traits develop later
- **General Crossbreeding Potential:** Cross-species compatible
- **Known Naturally Compatible:** Sahuagin + Sahuagin
- **Known Cross-Species Compatibility:** other completed internal-fertilization species under the global compatibility engine when no specific incompatibility exists
- **Known Conditional Pairings:** development requires aquatic egg incubation when the female is Sahuagin
- **Notes:** Sahuagin are an important example showing that a fishlike body plan does not automatically mean external fertilization.

## Malenti

- **Species:** Malenti
- **Source / Version:** Sahuagin mutation / Forgotten Realms and older D&D
- **Profile Status:** Complete by inheritance from Sahuagin
- **Reproductive Mode:** Sexual
- **Fertilization Method:** Internal
- **Development Method:** Egg-laying when female
- **Known Naturally Compatible:** Malenti + Malenti; Malenti + ordinary Sahuagin is source-supported
- **Known Incompatibility:** Malenti + Sea Elf is explicitly source-incompatible despite the Malenti's Sea-Elf-like appearance
- **Notes:** Visual resemblance never overrides a recorded biological incompatibility.

# Kuo-toa

## Kuo-toa

- **Species:** Kuo-toa
- **Source / Version:** Forgotten Realms / older D&D ecology
- **Creature Type:** Humanoid in modern D&D
- **Profile Status:** Complete — Conditional Crossbreeder
- **Evidence Basis:** Source lore explicitly states that Kuo-toa spawn like fish: females lay eggs in communal pools and males fertilize them afterward; source lore also records Human/Kuo-toa hybrid offspring
- **Compatibility Family:** External Aquatic Fishlike
- **Reproductive Mode:** Sexual
- **Male Reproductive Role:** releases the fertilizing biological contribution into the spawning environment after eggs are laid
- **Female Reproductive Role:** lays unfertilized eggs into the communal spawning environment
- **Fertilization Method:** External
- **Development Method:** Aquatic eggs followed by aquatic fingerling development before amphibious maturation
- **Carrier / Host:** No internal carrier after spawning
- **Special Biological Requirements:** compatible water environment, gamete viability, successful fertilization after laying, and aquatic juvenile development
- **Birth / Hatching Constraints:** offspring begins as aquatic egg/fingerling form; humanoid or other ancestry can emerge progressively during maturation
- **General Crossbreeding Potential:** Conditionally compatible
- **Known Naturally Compatible:** Kuo-toa + Kuo-toa when spawning requirements are met
- **Known Cross-Species Compatibility:** Human/Kuo-toa hybrid offspring are source-established as possible
- **Profile-specific Human bridge:** because source lore establishes Human/Kuo-toa hybrids, Human reproductive material is treated as biologically capable of participating in a Kuo-toa crossbreeding pathway when the directional conditions below are satisfied
- **Female Kuo-toa + Male Human:** conditionally compatible; female lays eggs and the male Human contribution must remain viable and reach the eggs in the required water environment
- **Female Human + Male Kuo-toa:** conditionally compatible; the male Kuo-toa contribution must be transferred internally and remain viable in Human internal fertilization, after which the Human female follows live-bearing development
- **Other Cross-Species Pairings:** require the ordinary external/internal compatibility test; Human evidence does not automatically clear every species
- **Notes:** This is a directional compatibility profile. The mechanism changes depending on which species is female.

# Bullywug

## Bullywug

- **Species:** Bullywug
- **Source / Version:** Forgotten Realms / older D&D ecology
- **Creature Type:** Humanoid
- **Profile Status:** Complete — Conditional Crossbreeder
- **Evidence Basis:** Source lore describes females laying large clutches in communal breeding ponds and tadpole-like young; older lore also contains reports/rumors of viable Bullywug/Human hybrids
- **Compatibility Family:** External Aquatic Amphibian
- **Reproductive Mode:** Sexual
- **Male Reproductive Role:** supplies the fertilizing biological contribution to eggs in the breeding environment
- **Female Reproductive Role:** lays eggs in the breeding pond
- **Fertilization Method:** External under the repository amphibian baseline
- **Development Method:** Eggs followed by tadpole/larval development and metamorphic juvenile growth
- **Carrier / Host:** No internal carrier after spawning
- **Special Biological Requirements:** suitable breeding water, viable gametes, and an environment that supports larval development
- **Birth / Hatching Constraints:** offspring begins as egg and larval form; adult hybrid anatomy develops through metamorphosis
- **General Crossbreeding Potential:** Conditionally compatible
- **Known Naturally Compatible:** Bullywug + Bullywug
- **Human bridge evidence:** older lore reports viable Bullywug/Human hybrids, so Human pairing is allowed as a profile-specific conditional bridge rather than dismissed solely because fertilization methods differ
- **Female Bullywug + Male Human:** conditionally compatible if the Human contribution remains viable in the breeding pond and can fertilize the laid eggs
- **Female Human + Male Bullywug:** conditionally compatible only when the male Bullywug contribution can be transferred internally and remain viable; this direction is not presumed from amphibian biology alone and must be explicitly supported by the applicable campaign/version or later homebrew refinement
- **Other Cross-Species Pairings:** require the standard external-fertilization compatibility test
- **Notes:** Bullywug reproduction demonstrates why compatibility can be direction-specific rather than a simple yes/no relationship between species names.

# Merfolk

## Merfolk

- **Species:** Merfolk
- **Source / Version:** General D&D / Forgotten Realms-style Merfolk
- **Creature Type:** Humanoid
- **Profile Status:** Complete under repository homebrew biological baseline
- **Evidence Basis:** Source lore establishes male/female Merfolk and source-recorded cross-species children with Sirines; exact ordinary fertilization/development is not consistently specified, so this repository establishes a stable internal live-bearing aquatic baseline
- **Compatibility Family:** Internal Live-Bearing Aquatic Humanoid
- **Reproductive Mode:** Sexual
- **Male Reproductive Role:** supplies the fertilizing biological contribution
- **Female Reproductive Role:** supplies the ovum-equivalent contribution and internally carries the developing offspring
- **Fertilization Method:** Internal
- **Development Method:** Live-bearing
- **Carrier / Host:** Female parent
- **Special Biological Requirements:** functioning internal reproductive anatomy; pregnancy remains physiologically supported in water
- **Birth / Hatching Constraints:** live-born young are immediately aquatic-capable enough to survive the maternal environment; fins, scales, tail proportions, and other hybrid traits can mature later
- **General Crossbreeding Potential:** Cross-species compatible
- **Known Naturally Compatible:** Merfolk + Merfolk
- **Known Cross-Species Evidence:** Merfolk + Sirine offspring with blended characteristics are source-recorded
- **Other Cross-Species Compatibility:** other completed internal-fertilization species under the global compatibility engine
- **Notes:** A different setting may define egg-laying Merfolk and should receive a separate version profile rather than silently replacing this one.

# Sahuagin / Sea-Elf visual mutation rule

A hybrid-looking phenotype is not proof of biological compatibility.

Malenti resemble Sea Elves but source lore records that Malenti cannot mate with Sea Elves. Therefore:

- appearance alone cannot establish compatibility
- environmental mutation can mimic another species without creating shared reproductive biology
- a profile-specific incompatibility overrides the general internal-fertilization cross-family default

---

# Directional compatibility rule

Batch 4 establishes the first explicit **directional crossbreeding** cases.

When Species A and Species B use different fertilization methods, these are separate questions:

- male A + female B
- male B + female A

One direction may work while the reverse direction does not.

The female determines where the ovum/egg begins and how development proceeds, while the male contribution must be capable of reaching and fertilizing it through the applicable pathway.

Do not collapse directional compatibility into a single symmetric yes/no field when the biology differs.
