# Reproductive Species Complete Profiles — Batch 7

## Purpose

This batch establishes stable biological hybrid lineages whose **historical origin was extraordinary**, but whose later populations can reproduce biologically through ordinary male/female sexual reproduction.

It reinforces this distinction:

> Extraordinary origin does not automatically mean magical-only reproduction forever.

A transformed, engineered, divine-touched, fiend-descended, or magically originated population can enter the current breeding pool **only when the profiled adult version has become a stable biological sexual lineage that satisfies the ordinary reproductive gate**.

# Extaminaar

## Extaminaar — stable Human/Yuan-ti hybrid lineage

- **Species:** Extaminaar
- **Source / Version:** Forgotten Realms / 3e-era stable Extaminaar lineage
- **Creature Type:** Humanoid
- **Profile Status:** Complete
- **Evidence Basis:** Source lore explicitly identifies Extaminaar as Human/Yuan-ti hybrids produced through crossbreeding programs, with later generations deliberately bred and tracked through at least a third generation
- **Parent Ancestries:** Human + Yuan-ti
- **Compatibility Family:** Internal Live-Bearing Humanoid for the stable Extaminaar population
- **Reproductive Mode:** Sexual
- **Male Reproductive Role:** supplies the fertilizing biological contribution
- **Female Reproductive Role:** supplies the ovum-equivalent contribution and carries the developing offspring
- **Fertilization Method:** Internal
- **Development Method:** Live-bearing under repository homebrew stable-lineage biology
- **Carrier / Host:** Female Extaminaar parent
- **Special Biological Requirements:** functioning internal-fertilization anatomy
- **Birth / Hatching Constraints:** newborn remains carrier-safe and generally humanoid; serpentine coloration, secretory glands, unusual skeletal density, scales, venom-related structures, or other Yuan-ti-derived traits can appear or mature later according to hybrid inheritance
- **General Crossbreeding Potential:** Cross-species compatible
- **Known Naturally Compatible:** Extaminaar + Extaminaar
- **Known Cross-Species Compatibility:** Human, Yuan-ti, and other completed internal-fertilization species under the global compatibility engine
- **Lineage Fertility Evidence:** source lore describes multigenerational Extaminaar breeding, so the stable lineage is fertile rather than merely a one-generation hybrid
- **Notes:** The original breeding program does not mean every later conception requires magic or experimentation. This completed profile represents the stable descendant population.

## Human/Yuan-ti compatibility evidence

Extaminaar provide direct setting evidence that Human and Yuan-ti biology can produce viable descendants in at least one D&D lineage.

Under this repository's directional-development rules:

- male Human + female Yuan-ti -> the Yuan-ti female follows her egg-laying biology unless the specific lineage profile says otherwise
- male Yuan-ti + female Human -> the Human female follows live-bearing biology
- the exact phenotype need not be identical to historical Extaminaar, because this repository's later inheritance system determines the child's traits

The existence of Extaminaar establishes parent compatibility. It does not force every Human/Yuan-ti child to use the Extaminaar name or statistics.

## Demonspawn Krinth — first-generation origin evidence

- **Species / lineage:** Demonspawn Krinth
- **Profile Status:** Hybrid-origin evidence, not automatic breeder profile for every possible parent fiend
- **Evidence Basis:** Source lore identifies original Krinth as direct offspring of a Shadow-associated demonic being and a Humanoid parent; the non-demonic ancestry could be Human, Dwarf, Orc, or another Humanoid
- **Purpose in this repository:** evidence that at least some version of the fiendish parent used in this lineage could produce biological offspring with multiple Humanoid peoples
- **Restriction:** this evidence does **not automatically admit every Shadow Demon, demon, or Fiend species into the breeding pool**. The actual fiend parent version must itself satisfy the current no-shapechanger, biological-sex, and workable-fertilization criteria before it can be selected as a breeder
- **Notes:** A first-generation hybrid can exist as source evidence even when one parent species still needs its own eligibility profile.

# Stable-lineage rule

A lineage with an extraordinary origin may become a normal biological breeder when all of the following are true:

1. later generations contain biologically male and female individuals
2. those individuals reproduce sexually
3. fertilization is biologically workable
4. offspring develop through a viable biological route
5. reproduction no longer requires recreating the original transformation, ritual, curse, divine intervention, laboratory procedure, or magical event for every conception

When those conditions are met, profile the **stable descendant lineage** separately from its extraordinary origin state.

This rule can apply to future candidates such as:

- magically originated peoples that later breed true
- engineered species with stable heritable biology
- divine or fiend-touched lineages that reproduce biologically after establishment
- science-fantasy created species whose descendants reproduce sexually

It does not admit a species whose every new member must still be individually created or transformed by magic.

---

# Hybrid lineage names are not mandatory outcomes

Extaminaar and Krinth demonstrate historical lineage names, but repository hybrid children are not forced into an existing named race merely because their parents match an old source pairing.

When the later inheritance system is active:

- an old named lineage can be selected or emerge when appropriate
- a new phenotype can instead result from the same parent ancestries
- parent direction can change developmental expression
- campaign-specific bloodlines can stabilize into new reusable species profiles

The historical lineage proves compatibility more strongly than it dictates phenotype.
