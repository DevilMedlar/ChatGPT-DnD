# Playable Character Options

## Purpose and authority

This file is the repository-wide authority for:

- species / ancestry options available during PC character creation
- the complete playable mechanics of those approved species
- the same approved species roster used by `REPRODUCTION_AND_LINEAGE.md`
- the default official-class, subclass, background, feat, multiclassing, and homebrew-option policy

`CHARACTER_CREATION.md` owns the workflow for building and finalizing characters. `CORE_GAME_MECHANICS.md` owns the general D&D 5.5e rules baseline. `REPRODUCTION_AND_LINEAGE.md` owns compatibility, conception, pregnancy or egg development, hybrid inheritance, birth or hatching, and later-generation rules.

A species or character option omitted from this file is not globally selectable merely because it appears in an official builder, another sourcebook, a deleted repository file, Git history, a prior chat, or remembered context.

An omitted option may become available only through:

1. the player's newer explicit global instruction that updates this file, or
2. an explicit campaign-specific override in `campaigns/campaign-N/Rules/Campaign-N_Rules.md`.

A campaign-specific addition applies only to that campaign.

## Shared character-option rules

### Starting level and ability scores

- Characters begin at Level 1 unless an explicit campaign rule establishes another starting level.
- Ability scores use `CORE_GAME_MECHANICS.md`.
- Species never supplies ability-score increases. Under the 5.5e baseline, ordinary ability-score adjustments come from Background.
- A species package cannot be stacked with another species package, an older version of the same species, or a monster stat block.

### Adult and maturity requirement

Every PC must be at least 18 years old. A PC participating in adult or reproductive content must also be physically and reproductively mature.

For this homebrew roster, all standard selectable species can reach physical adulthood by age 18. Longer-lived species may have later cultural milestones, but those traditions do not make an 18+ physically mature character a child.

### Languages

Use the current 5.5e character-creation language rules unless a specific trait below says otherwise. A species name does not automatically grant every language associated with all of its published settings.

### Trait DC

When a species trait calls for a saving throw, use:

`Species Save DC = 8 + Proficiency Bonus + the ability modifier named by the trait`

When the trait permits a choice of Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma, choose once when the character is created.

### Rest and use recovery

A trait that says it can be used a number of times equal to Proficiency Bonus regains all expended uses after a Long Rest unless the trait explicitly says otherwise.

### Standard forms only

Each entry below is one approved standard species package.

- Subraces, setting branches, color groups, alternate lineages, third-party versions, templates, and transformed states are not automatically available.
- Cosmetic choices inside a standard package do not create a new species.
- A class, subclass, background, profession, title, faction, culture, or disguise is not a species.
- A hybrid is not automatically a selectable species. Hybrid character creation requires explicit approval under `REPRODUCTION_AND_LINEAGE.md`.

# Species packages

## Human

- **Creature Type:** Humanoid
- **Size:** Medium
- **Speed:** 30 feet
- **Typical Lifespan:** About 80 years
- **Body Plan:** Standard human anatomy and appearance
- **Reproductive Baseline:** Internal fertilization; female live-bearing

### Resourceful

Whenever you finish a Long Rest, you gain Heroic Inspiration.

### Skillful

You gain proficiency in one skill of your choice.

### Versatile

You gain one Origin feat of your choice for which you qualify.

### Balance note

This package is the standard 5.5e Human identity: flexible skill and feat access rather than a fixed supernatural power set.

## Elf

- **Creature Type:** Humanoid
- **Size:** Medium
- **Speed:** 30 feet
- **Typical Lifespan:** About 750 years
- **Body Plan:** Slender humanlike body, pointed ears, and the standard Elf appearance established during character creation
- **Reproductive Baseline:** Internal fertilization; female live-bearing

### Darkvision

You have Darkvision with a range of 60 feet.

### Fey Ancestry

You have Advantage on saving throws you make to avoid or end the Charmed condition.

### Keen Senses

Choose Insight, Perception, or Survival. You gain proficiency in the chosen skill.

### Trance

You do not need to sleep, and magic cannot put you to sleep. You can finish a Long Rest in 4 hours by spending those hours in conscious trancelike meditation.

### Standard-package rule

This is the single standard Elf package. Drow, High Elf, Wood Elf, Sea Elf, Astral Elf, Eladrin, and other branches are not silently substituted and do not add lineage spells or other traits.

## Dwarf

- **Creature Type:** Humanoid
- **Size:** Medium
- **Speed:** 30 feet
- **Typical Lifespan:** About 350 years
- **Body Plan:** Compact, sturdy humanoid body
- **Reproductive Baseline:** Internal fertilization; female live-bearing

### Darkvision

You have Darkvision with a range of 120 feet.

### Dwarven Resilience

You have Resistance to Poison damage. You also have Advantage on saving throws you make to avoid or end the Poisoned condition.

### Dwarven Toughness

Your Hit Point maximum increases by 1 when you select this species and by 1 whenever you gain a character level.

### Stonecunning

As a Bonus Action while touching a natural or worked stone surface, you gain Tremorsense with a range of 60 feet for 10 minutes. You can use this trait a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus and regain all uses after a Long Rest.

## Gnome

- **Creature Type:** Humanoid
- **Size:** Small
- **Speed:** 30 feet
- **Typical Lifespan:** About 425 years
- **Body Plan:** Small humanoid body with the standard Gnome appearance established during character creation
- **Reproductive Baseline:** Internal fertilization; female live-bearing

### Darkvision

You have Darkvision with a range of 60 feet.

### Gnomish Cunning

You have Advantage on Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma saving throws.

### Gnomecraft

You know the Mending and Prestidigitation cantrips. Choose Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma as the spellcasting ability for these cantrips.

### Standard-package rule

This package replaces branch selection. Forest Gnome, Rock Gnome, Deep Gnome, and other branches are not separately available and do not add further traits unless explicitly approved.

## Dragonborn

- **Creature Type:** Humanoid
- **Size:** Medium
- **Speed:** 30 feet
- **Typical Lifespan:** About 100 years
- **Body Plan:** Wingless bipedal draconic humanoid with scales, horns, and other standard draconic features
- **Reproductive Baseline:** Internal fertilization; female produces and lays fertilized eggs

### Draconic Energy

Choose Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, or Poison when the character is created. This is a trait setting, not a separate species variation.

### Breath Weapon

When you take the Attack action, you can replace one attack with a 15-foot Cone or a 30-foot-long, 5-foot-wide Line. Creatures in the area make a Dexterity saving throw against:

`8 + Constitution modifier + Proficiency Bonus`

On a failed save, a creature takes `1d10` damage of your Draconic Energy type; on a success, it takes half damage.

The damage becomes `2d10` at character level 5, `3d10` at level 11, and `4d10` at level 17.

You can use this trait a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus and regain all uses after a Long Rest.

### Damage Resistance

You have Resistance to the damage type selected for Draconic Energy.

### Darkvision

You have Darkvision with a range of 60 feet.

### Draconic Flight

At character level 5, you can use a Bonus Action to manifest spectral wings for 10 minutes. During that time, you have a Fly Speed equal to your Speed. The effect ends early if you dismiss it, become Incapacitated, or die.

Once used, this trait cannot be used again until you finish a Long Rest.

## Dragonkin

- **Creature Type:** Humanoid
- **Size:** Medium
- **Speed:** 30 feet
- **Typical Lifespan:** About 200 years
- **Body Plan:** Fixed winged draconic humanoid with scales, claws, horns, and tail; not a Dragonborn branch or class
- **Reproductive Baseline:** Internal fertilization; female produces and lays fertilized eggs

### Darkvision

You have Darkvision with a range of 60 feet.

### Draconic Claws

Your claws are natural weapons. You can use Strength or Dexterity for their attack and damage rolls, and they deal `1d6` Slashing damage on a hit.

### Wing-Assisted Descent

When you fall at least 10 feet while conscious and able to move your wings, you can use your Reaction to reduce the falling damage by `5 × your Proficiency Bonus`. Before landing, you may also move horizontally up to a number of feet equal to your Speed, provided there is open space.

### Mature Flight

At character level 5, you can use a Bonus Action to gain a Fly Speed equal to your Speed for 10 minutes. You cannot use this flight while wearing Medium or Heavy armor.

Once used, this trait cannot be used again until you finish a Long Rest.

### Draconic Build

You count as one size larger when determining your carrying capacity and the weight you can push, drag, or lift.

## True Dragon

- **Creature Type:** Dragon
- **Size:** Medium in the adventuring-scale form used by this species package
- **Speed:** 30 feet
- **Typical Lifespan:** About 1,200 years
- **Body Plan:** True draconic body plus an innate compact or Humanoid accommodation form
- **Reproductive Baseline:** Internal fertilization; a female True Dragon produces and lays fertilized eggs; another female species uses her own route

### Adventuring-Scale Rule

Selecting True Dragon never grants a monster stat block, Legendary Actions, Legendary Resistance, Lair Actions, monster Hit Points, monster Challenge Rating, or automatic adult-monster size.

The character uses normal class levels, Hit Dice, ability scores, proficiency, equipment rules, and action economy.

### Draconic Essence

Choose Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, or Poison when the character is created.

You have Resistance to the chosen damage type.

### Lesser Breath Weapon

When you take the Attack action, you can replace one attack with a 15-foot Cone or a 30-foot-long, 5-foot-wide Line. Creatures in the area make a Dexterity saving throw against:

`8 + Constitution modifier + Proficiency Bonus`

On a failed save, a creature takes `1d8` damage of your Draconic Essence type; on a success, it takes half damage.

The damage becomes `2d8` at character level 5, `3d8` at level 11, and `4d8` at level 17.

You can use this trait a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus and regain all uses after a Long Rest.

### Darkvision

You have Darkvision with a range of 60 feet.

### Draconic Form

In your compact true-dragon form, your claws are natural weapons that deal `1d6` Slashing damage and can use Strength or Dexterity for attack and damage rolls.

### Form Accommodation

As an action, you can change between:

- your Medium compact true-dragon form, and
- a Small or Medium Humanoid form chosen when you first use this trait.

Your game statistics, Hit Points, class features, ability scores, equipment ownership, ancestry, biological sex, and fertility do not change. Equipment merges into the new form or reappears when you return, at your choice when transforming; merged equipment provides no benefit.

The Humanoid form cannot use Draconic Claws or physical wings. The change is not an illusion and requires no spell, item, Concentration, or roll.

For reproductive physical access, a mature True Dragon can also safely adjust to a compatible size or functional Humanoid anatomy as described in `REPRODUCTION_AND_LINEAGE.md`. This never changes parentage or the female development route.

### Developing Flight

At character level 5, while in true-dragon form, you can use a Bonus Action to gain a Fly Speed equal to your Speed for 10 minutes. Once used, you cannot do so again until a Long Rest.

At level 11, the duration becomes 1 hour.

At level 17, you have a permanent Fly Speed equal to your Speed while in true-dragon form.

## Kemonomimi standard

Kitsune, Neko, Usagi, Inu, and Ookami are fixed biological species with a humanlike face, skin, eyes, torso, arms, hands, legs, and feet; species-appropriate animal ears on top of the head; and a matching tail.

They do not have muzzles, snouts, paws, or full-body fur. Small human ears may also be visible as a cosmetic choice. Ears and tails are real biological structures and may react involuntarily to emotion.

Animal sounds, gestures, habits, and mannerisms are optional characterization. They do not dictate intelligence, alignment, class, consent, personality, or behavior.

## Kitsune

- **Creature Type:** Humanoid
- **Size:** Medium
- **Speed:** 30 feet
- **Typical Lifespan:** About 150 years
- **Body Plan:** Human-bodied Kemonomimi with fox ears and one fox tail at character creation
- **Reproductive Baseline:** Internal fertilization; female live-bearing

### Darkvision

You have Darkvision with a range of 60 feet.

### Keen Hearing and Smell

You have Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on hearing or smell and on Wisdom (Survival) checks made to follow a scent trail.

### Fox Guile

Choose Deception or Insight. You gain proficiency in the chosen skill.

### Fox Step

As a Bonus Action, you can take the Disengage action. You can use this trait a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus and regain all uses after a Long Rest.

### Tail Growth

Additional tails may develop through age, story, or a specific feature, but extra tails are cosmetic unless an explicit mechanic says otherwise. They do not create extra attacks, actions, spell slots, or reproductive roles.

## Lizardfolk

# Class policy

## Core official classes

The following twelve 5.5e core classes are globally available:

1. Barbarian
2. Bard
3. Cleric
4. Druid
5. Fighter
6. Monk
7. Paladin
8. Ranger
9. Rogue
10. Sorcerer
11. Warlock
12. Wizard

Every approved species may choose any approved class. Species does not impose a class, alignment, occupation, personality, spell list, or narrative role.

Use the current official 5.5e / SRD 5.2.1 class rules rather than copying the complete level-by-level class text into this repository.

## Subclasses

- Use a current official 5.5e subclass when its complete mechanics are lawfully available to the player and can be verified.
- A subclass is not selectable from its name or flavor alone.
- If its complete mechanics cannot be verified, leave the subclass undecided or use an approved alternative rather than inventing missing features.
- A campaign-specific subclass restriction or addition belongs in that campaign's local rule file.

## Additional official classes

An official class outside the twelve core classes is not globally enabled by implication. It may be added globally by the player's explicit instruction or locally through a campaign-specific override after its complete current mechanics can be verified.

## Homebrew class completion gate

A homebrew class is selectable only after its rules establish at minimum:

- class name and concept
- primary ability
- Hit Point Die
- saving throw proficiencies
- skill, weapon, tool, and armor proficiencies
- starting equipment or starting gold
- a complete level 1–20 feature table
- every feature's exact action type, range, target, duration, uses, recharge, scaling, and stacking rules
- spellcasting progression and spell list when applicable
- subclass selection level and full subclass feature progression
- multiclass prerequisite, gained proficiencies, and interaction with spell slots or class resources
- capstone and high-level scaling
- any required formulas and saving throw DCs

A class name, concept paragraph, or partial feature list is not a completed class.

## Multiclassing

Use the current official 5.5e multiclassing rules. A homebrew class must define its multiclass prerequisite and gained proficiencies before multiclassing into or out of it is allowed.

# Background, feat, and equipment policy

## Backgrounds

Official 5.5e backgrounds are available by default when their complete current mechanics can be verified.

A homebrew background must define:

- three thematically appropriate ability scores from which its increases are assigned under the 5.5e rules
- one Origin feat
- two skill proficiencies
- one tool proficiency
- starting equipment or starting gold
- a concise narrative premise

## Feats

Use current official 5.5e feat prerequisites, repeatability, level requirements, and effects. A homebrew feat requires complete mechanics and explicit approval.

## Starting equipment

Starting equipment comes from the selected class and background unless the player chooses an allowed starting-gold option or an explicit campaign rule replaces it. Record all finalized equipment and currency in `inventory.md`.
