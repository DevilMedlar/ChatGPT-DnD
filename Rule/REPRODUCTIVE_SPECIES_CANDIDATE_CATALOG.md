# Reproductive Species Candidate Catalog

## Purpose

This file is the repository-wide **compressed candidate registry** for species that may fit the biological breeding rules in `REPRODUCTION_AND_LINEAGE.md` and `REPRODUCTIVE_SPECIES_PROFILES.md`.

The acceptance test remains:

> **Sexual reproduction + biologically male/female reproductive roles + workable fertilization + viable development + a Naturally compatible, Cross-species compatible, or actually satisfied Conditionally compatible pairing = biologically eligible.**

This catalog is intentionally **not exhaustive**. It is a compact set of representative biological species/ancestry/race families. A species omitted from this list is not banned; it may be researched and added later if it passes the same biological gate.

## Compression rule

Keep the catalog small enough to use during play.

1. Keep **one canonical row per biological species/family** whenever variants share the same reproductive biology.
2. Keep **no more than 5 named representative variants/examples** under one canonical species/family.
3. Regional populations, cultures, nations, clans, houses, subcultures, and setting labels with unchanged biology are **not separate species rows**.
4. A class, subclass, profession, caste, order, title, archetype, transformation state, or job is **not a species row** unless it creates a stable inheritable biological population with materially different reproduction.
5. Hybrid labels that merely describe a parent pairing are normally handled by the repository's hybrid system instead of being permanent catalog species.
6. Keep a separate version only when its biology, lifecycle, fertilization, development, or fertility materially differs.
7. When more than 5 variants exist, retain the most biologically representative or mechanically useful examples and record the rest only in completed profiles if needed.

## Status meanings

- **Candidate**: sexual male/female reproduction is established or strongly biologically supported.
- **Candidate — conditional**: reproduction plausibly works but depends on a specific biological/environmental condition.
- **Hold**: a real reproductive fact is still unresolved.
- **Not a breeder under current global rules**: the known lifecycle conflicts with the current restricted breeding gate.

# Meaningful Holds

Keep only unresolved species where an actual missing biological fact matters.

| Species / ancestry | Why it remains Hold |
|---|---|
| Cnidaran | Sexual communal lifecycle is known, but stable biologically male/female roles are not established |
| Feathren | Known to be born, but live-bearing versus egg-laying and stable sex roles remain unresolved |
| Etherean | Extraplanar reproductive biology remains unresolved |
| Gloura | Fey reproductive biology remains unresolved |
| Ombrask | Transformed adult state; source does not establish whether reproductive function survives transformation |

Additional obscure species should **not** be kept as permanent Holds merely to preserve a long list. Research them when they become relevant.

# Not breeders under the current global gate

Do not maintain a giant species-by-species exclusion table. The following categories are enough to resolve ordinary cases:

- constructed-only peoples such as Warforged, Autognomes, Modrons, artificial dolls/puppets, and similar created bodies
- undead-only or corpse-dependent lineages when new individuals are not produced through male/female sexual reproduction
- asexual-only species such as Heliana Oozekin under their published self-replication lifecycle
- fungal/spore propagation species such as Mycelian when no male/female sexual system exists
- shapechanger species under the current global no-shapechanger rule
- transformation-only lineages such as Kaorti or Anakore when propagation requires converting another creature
- created-from-existing-person lineages such as Elan under versions where new individuals are made rather than born
- species whose reproductive sex roles are temporary/sequential rather than stable male/female roles, such as Dromite under the current global restriction
- magical-manifestation, reincarnation-only, summoned, or individually created beings with no stable biological reproductive population

Named excluded species can remain documented in their completed/resolution profile batches. They do not need permanent rows in this candidate catalog.

# Removed as non-species catalog entries

The following kinds of entries should not return as standalone reproductive species unless later evidence establishes materially different inheritable biology:

- regional Human populations such as Thoric, Varan, Calidian, Antilan, Abbalaya, and original Khala
- Human/Half-Elf cultural groups such as Aperusa
- Dhakaani social/national labels when they are biologically ordinary Goblins, Hobgoblins, or Bugbears
- class, subclass, profession, order, title, caste, guild, cult, military role, magical discipline, or adventuring archetype
- vague placeholders such as `Pangolin-like peoples when sapient`, `Peryton-like sapient people if established`, or other hypothetical unnamed species
- temporary transformations and templates that do not create a stable breeding population

# Automatic future intake rule

Whenever a new species appears, first determine whether it deserves a **new canonical biological row**.

1. Is it actually a distinct inheritable biological species/ancestry/race rather than a culture, class, caste, title, or regional population?
2. Does it normally reproduce sexually?
3. Does it have stable biologically male and female reproductive roles under the current global scope?
4. Is there a workable internal, external, implanted, or other non-magical fertilization path?
5. Is there a viable live-bearing, egg-laying, host-dependent, aquatic, larval, or other biological development path?
6. Does an existing canonical family already describe the same biology?

If an existing family fits, add the new name only as a variant/example and keep no more than 5 representative variants in the candidate catalog.

If the biology materially differs, create a new canonical row/profile.

If uncertain, research it when relevant rather than permanently bloating the candidate list.
