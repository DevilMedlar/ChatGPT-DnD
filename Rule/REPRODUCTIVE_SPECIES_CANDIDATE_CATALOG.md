# Reproductive Species Candidate Catalog

## Purpose and authority

This file is the repository-wide **active global whitelist** for species and biological families selectable in the homebrew crossbreeding system.

`REPRODUCTION_AND_LINEAGE.md` governs how reproduction works. `REPRODUCTIVE_SPECIES_COMPLETE_PROFILES.md` supplies the reproductive biology for every entry below.

A species or family omitted from this whitelist is not globally selectable merely because it appears in an older source, deleted file, Git history, remembered chat context, or surviving background research.

An omitted or new species may enter only through:

1. the player's newer explicit global instruction, or
2. an applicable campaign-specific override.

## Biological admission gate

Every retained entry must have:

- stable biological males
- stable biological females
- physical male/female mating as the reproductive act
- internal biological fertilization
- viable live-bearing or egg-laying development

The global whitelist does not include external spawners, asexual species, hermaphroditic or sequential-sex species, constructed or magically created peoples, transformation-dependent reproduction, or species whose reproductive anatomy remains unresolved.

Kitsune are a specific approved natural-body exception described below and in their complete profile.

## Catalog compression rules

1. Keep one canonical row per biological species or family when variants share the same reproductive biology.
2. Keep no more than five representative variants or groups beneath one row.
3. A culture, nation, clan, house, caste, class, subclass, profession, title, order, archetype, or temporary transformation is not a separate species.
4. A hybrid label describing parentage is handled by the hybrid rules unless the player explicitly establishes it as a stable selectable lineage.
5. Keep a separate version only when its reproductive biology materially differs.
6. Passing the biological gate does not automatically restore a species the player removed from this whitelist.

### Kitsune natural-body limitation

- Reproductive anatomy comes from the stable natural Kitsune body.
- Disguises, illusions, alternate appearances, and assumed forms do not create reproductive anatomy or compatibility.
- Shapechanging is not required for Kitsune reproduction.
- This exception does not restore any other removed shapechanger species.

## Selection rules

- Offer these ten entries for ordinary global character and NPC reproductive-species selection.
- Show representative variants beneath the parent row rather than treating them as separate reproductive species.
- Consult `REPRODUCTIVE_SPECIES_COMPLETE_PROFILES.md` for biology.
- Consult `REPRODUCTION_AND_LINEAGE.md` for compatibility, directional development, birth safety, adoption, and hybrid handling.
- Do not offer a removed species through an older profile, historical commit, deleted file, or remembered conversation.
- Campaign-specific additions belong in that campaign's local rule file and do not change the global whitelist.

## Adding a future entry

Before adding a new canonical row, verify that:

1. the player explicitly approved the global addition or the species belongs to an already retained family
2. it is a distinct inheritable biological species rather than a culture, class, role, or temporary state
3. it has stable biological males and females
4. those males and females physically mate for reproduction
5. mating provides internal fertilization
6. the offspring has a viable biological development route
7. an existing row does not already cover the same reproductive biology

When an existing family fits, add the name only as a representative variant when useful and preserve the five-variant limit.
