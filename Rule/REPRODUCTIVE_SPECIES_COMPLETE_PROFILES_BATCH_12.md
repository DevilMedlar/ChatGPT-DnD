# Reproductive Species Complete Profiles — Batch 12

## Purpose

This batch resolves stable Eberron and psionic peoples whose source material provides enough biological information to move beyond Candidate/Hold status.

It follows the global biological gate in `REPRODUCTION_AND_LINEAGE.md`, the fertilization engine in `REPRODUCTIVE_FERTILIZATION_COMPATIBILITY.md`, and the version/family rules in `REPRODUCTIVE_SPECIES_PROFILE_INDEX.md`.

A specific determination here overrides an older generic Candidate or Hold row for the same version.

# Kalashtar

- **Species:** Kalashtar
- **Source / Version:** Eberron, especially `Races of Eberron`
- **Creature Type:** Humanoid in the biological Eberron lineage; later mechanics may label the species differently by edition
- **Profile Status:** Complete
- **Evidence Basis:** Source-established newborns, biological descent, procreation, and interbreeding with Humans and Half-Elves
- **Compatibility Family:** Internal Live-Bearing Humanoid / Psionic-Quori Lineage
- **Reproductive Mode:** Sexual
- **Male Reproductive Role:** supplies the fertilizing biological contribution
- **Female Reproductive Role:** supplies the ovum-equivalent contribution and carries the developing offspring
- **Fertilization Method:** Internal
- **Development Method:** Live-bearing
- **Carrier / Host:** Female parent
- **Special Biological Requirements:** functioning compatible internal-fertilization anatomy; the quori spiritual bond is inherited separately from the physical conception mechanism
- **Birth / Hatching Constraints:** ordinary live-birth constraints under the carrying female's viable range
- **General Crossbreeding Potential:** Cross-species compatible
- **Known Naturally Compatible:** Kalashtar + Kalashtar
- **Known Source-Established Cross-Species Compatibility:** Human; Half-Elf / Khoravar
- **Known Homebrew Cross-Species Compatibility:** other completed internal-fertilization species may be evaluated normally when anatomy is workable and no specific incompatibility exists
- **Source-Specific Lineage Expression:** in the classic `Races of Eberron` rule, a child whose sex matches the Kalashtar parent's quori-line inheritance is born Kalashtar; otherwise the child matches the non-Kalashtar parent's species. There is no classic half-Kalashtar category.
- **Notes:** The quori bond changes lineage expression but does not make conception itself magical. The later repository hybrid-inheritance system may establish a broader homebrew phenotype rule while retaining the source rule as version-specific precedent.

# Athasian Half-Giant

- **Species:** Half-Giant
- **Source / Version:** AD&D 2e Revised `Dark Sun Campaign Setting`
- **Creature Type:** Giant / Humanoid-derived stable hybrid lineage, edition-dependent
- **Profile Status:** Complete
- **Evidence Basis:** Source explicitly states that all modern Half-Giants descend from the original magically created Half-Giants and can produce offspring with other Half-Giants
- **Compatibility Family:** Internal Live-Bearing Giant / Stable Hybrid Giant
- **Reproductive Mode:** Sexual
- **Male Reproductive Role:** supplies the fertilizing biological contribution
- **Female Reproductive Role:** supplies the ovum-equivalent contribution and carries the developing offspring
- **Fertilization Method:** Internal
- **Development Method:** Live-bearing under the repository biological baseline
- **Carrier / Host:** Female parent
- **Special Biological Requirements:** functioning compatible Half-Giant internal-fertilization anatomy
- **Birth / Hatching Constraints:** offspring remain within the Half-Giant female's viable prenatal and delivery range; immense adult stature develops after birth
- **General Crossbreeding Potential:** Species-restricted biological compatibility in this source version
- **Known Naturally Compatible:** Half-Giant + Half-Giant
- **Known Source-Established Incompatibilities:** Human; Giant. The revised Athasian source explicitly says modern Half-Giants cannot naturally reproduce with either parent species.
- **Known Cross-Species Compatibility:** none automatically established for this specific Athasian version; any other pairing requires an explicit homebrew or campaign override rather than the generic internal-fertilization default
- **Notes:** This is a key exception to the general cross-family default. Magical creation of the founding population did not prevent the descendants from becoming a stable sexually reproducing population, but the source preserves a narrow breeding boundary.

# Resolved exclusion

## Dromite — Not a breeder under the current restricted global scope

- **Species:** Dromite
- **Source / Version:** D&D 3.5 `Expanded Psionics Handbook`
- **Status:** Ineligible under the current global breeding scope
- **Source-Established Reproduction:** a city-hive elects one Grand Queen who assumes female sexual characteristics and one Elected Consort who assumes male sexual characteristics; together they provide the hive's eggs for the year
- **Reason for Exclusion:** ordinary Dromites are genderless and possess no sexual characteristics. Reproductive sex is a temporary specialized caste state acquired through a communal ceremony rather than stable male/female reproductive biology across the species.
- **Failed Gate:** the current global restriction against sequential, temporary, or otherwise non-stable reproductive-sex systems
- **Future Override:** if the player later broadens the allowed reproductive-role system, Dromites are excellent candidates for a specialized internal-fertilization egg-laying profile.

## Stable extraordinary-origin rule reinforced

Kalashtar and Athasian Half-Giants reinforce the existing stable-lineage rule:

> An extraordinary founding event does not make every descendant magically reproduced.

Once a population has a stable male/female biological reproductive lifecycle, it can qualify under the global system even if the first generation came from a quori fusion, arcane experiment, planar event, divine intervention, or other extraordinary origin.

The reproductive profile must describe the **current stable population**, while the founding event remains ancestry/history unless each new child still requires that event.
