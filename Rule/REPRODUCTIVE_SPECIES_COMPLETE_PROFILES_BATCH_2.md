# Reproductive Species Complete Profiles — Batch 2

## Purpose

This file adds completed reproductive biology profiles for additional species from `REPRODUCTIVE_SPECIES_CANDIDATE_CATALOG.md`.

The global fertilization interaction rules in `REPRODUCTIVE_FERTILIZATION_COMPATIBILITY.md` apply to every profile here.

Unless a row says otherwise:

- same-species pairings are `Naturally compatible`
- different completed internal-fertilization species are `Cross-species compatible` when the global internal-fertilization test passes
- the biologically female contributor determines live-bearing versus egg-laying development
- prenatal or pre-laying size follows the carrier's safe range
- difficult inherited structures may develop after birth or hatching
- fertility percentages and conception chance remain undefined until the dedicated fertility system is built

## Shared profile: Internal Live-Bearing Humanoid

For species assigned to this shared profile:

- **Profile Status:** Complete
- **Reproductive Mode:** Sexual
- **Male Reproductive Role:** supplies the fertilizing biological contribution
- **Female Reproductive Role:** supplies the ovum-equivalent contribution and carries the developing offspring
- **Fertilization Method:** Internal
- **Development Method:** Live-bearing
- **Carrier / Host:** Female parent
- **Special Biological Requirements:** functioning compatible internal-fertilization anatomy
- **General Crossbreeding Potential:** Cross-species compatible
- **Birth Constraints:** carrier-safe live birth; larger size, horns, heavy armor, wings, or other difficult hybrid features develop later when required

## Grung

- **Species:** Grung
- **Source / Version:** One Grung Above
- **Creature Type:** Humanoid
- **Profile Status:** Complete — Conditional Crossbreeder
- **Evidence Basis:** amphibian body plan plus homebrew biological baseline
- **Compatibility Family:** External Aquatic Amphibian
- **Reproductive Mode:** Sexual
- **Male Reproductive Role:** releases the fertilizing biological contribution into the reproductive water environment
- **Female Reproductive Role:** releases eggs/ova into the reproductive water environment
- **Fertilization Method:** External
- **Development Method:** Eggs followed by aquatic larval development before maturation
- **Carrier / Host:** No internal carrier after spawning
- **Special Biological Requirements:** suitable fresh water, compatible temperature/chemistry, viable gametes in the shared environment, and successful larval development conditions
- **Birth / Hatching Constraints:** offspring begins as egg/larval form; hybrid adult traits may emerge through metamorphic development
- **General Crossbreeding Potential:** Conditionally compatible
- **Known Naturally Compatible:** Grung + Grung when environmental requirements are met
- **Known Cross-Species Compatibility:** another external-fertilization species only when both profiles' reproductive material can remain viable in the same environment
- **Known Conditional Pairings:** internal-fertilization species require an explicit external-to-internal biological bridge under `REPRODUCTIVE_FERTILIZATION_COMPATIBILITY.md`
- **Notes:** Grung is not automatically compatible with Human or other internal-fertilization species merely because both are sexual.

## Batch-2 completion summary

The species and lineages above no longer need to remain merely `Candidate` for their **basic reproductive method**. Their pair-by-pair compatibility still follows the global fertilization engine and any later specific incompatibility profile.

Future batches should prioritize:

1. aquatic sexual species with uncertain live-bearing versus egg-spawning biology
2. Yuan-ti and other serpentine lineages with multiple body forms
3. true giants and giantkin
4. additional true dragons and unusual dragon types
5. fiend-cross biological lineages such as Cambion, Fey'ri, and Tanarukk
6. older-edition Forgotten Realms and Spelljammer peoples
7. partner/homebrew biological species already listed in the candidate catalog
