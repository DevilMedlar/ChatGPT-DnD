# Reproductive Species Complete Profiles — Batch 9

## Purpose

This batch promotes fixed humanoid branches and stable mixed-heritage peoples whose reproductive biology is already covered by completed parent-family profiles.

It uses the same family-inheritance rule established in Batch 8, but for humanoid lineages rather than obvious animalfolk.

A branch listed here is `Complete by family inheritance` for basic reproductive method unless a campaign or specific source establishes materially different biology.

# Stable lineage vs transformation reminder

A stable biological descendant population can be Complete even when its distant origin involved transformation, mutation, planar influence, or magical engineering.

The deciding question is how **new members are normally produced now**.

If ordinary adult members reproduce sexually through biological male/female roles and viable development, they can enter the breeding pool.

If every new member still requires transformation, ritual creation, possession, construction, or magical spawning, that version remains outside the current initial pool.
