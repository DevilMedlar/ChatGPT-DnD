# Reproductive Species Complete Profiles — Batch 15

## Purpose

This batch resolves several Astromundi human populations and partner-setting species whose lifecycle information is clear enough to promote, exclude, or retain as meaningful Holds.

---

# Astromundi human-family promotions

The following are distinct cultures or human populations rather than reproductively separate species. They inherit the completed Human reproductive profile unless a campaign explicitly creates a divergent biological version.

## Thoric

- **Species / Population:** Thoric
- **Source / Version:** Spelljammer, `The Astromundi Cluster`
- **Creature Type:** Humanoid (Human)
- **Profile Status:** Complete by family inheritance
- **Inherited Profile:** Human / Internal Live-Bearing Humanoid
- **Evidence Basis:** Source identifies Thoric as a Human race/population and explicitly describes men, women, children, and babies
- **Reproductive Mode:** Sexual
- **Fertilization Method:** Internal
- **Development Method:** Live-bearing
- **Carrier / Host:** Female parent
- **General Crossbreeding Potential:** Human-family compatibility plus the normal global internal-fertilization engine
- **Notes:** Thoric height, build, culture, and environmental adaptation do not create a separate reproductive system.

# Resolved non-biological lifecycles

## Dara — Not a breeder under current global rules

- **Species:** Dara
- **Source / Version:** `Obojima: Tales from the Tall Grass`
- **Status:** Ineligible under the current global breeding scope
- **Source-Established Lifecycle:** when a Dara nears death, it places its remaining soul, memories, and identity into a parting glyph on a tree. Later, a new Dara emerges fully formed from the earth through the roots of that glyph-bearing tree.
- **Reason for Exclusion:** new Dara do not require a biologically male parent, biologically female parent, fertilization, pregnancy, egg, or other sexual reproductive pathway
- **Failed Gate:** sexual male/female reproduction
- **Future Override:** a campaign can create a separate biological Dara variant, but the published lifecycle itself does not qualify.

## Geleton — Not a breeder under current global rules

- **Species:** Geleton
- **Source / Version:** `The Griffon's Saddlebag: Book Two`
- **Status:** Ineligible under the current global breeding scope
- **Source-Established Lifecycle:** a Geleton arises through Synthesis, a spontaneous or magical symbiotic fusion between an ooze and a reanimated skeleton; Geletons come to life fully matured
- **Reason for Exclusion:** the species is formed by symbiotic magical creation rather than ordinary biological male/female sexual reproduction
- **Failed Gate:** biological conception and development
- **Future Override:** a stable descendant Geleton population with an independently established sexual reproductive system could receive a separate version profile.

# Birth evidence without proven adult breeder status

## Obojima Elf — Hold as adult breeder; Human-parent birth path established

- **Species / Lineage:** Obojima Elf
- **Source / Version:** `Obojima: Tales from the Tall Grass`
- **Profile Status:** Hold as an adult breeder
- **Source-Established Birth Path:** Obojima Elves are born from Human parents rather than constituting an ordinary independently reproducing species in the usual sense
- **Parent Pair Evidence:** Human parentage can produce an Obojima Elf child through the setting's unusual lineage expression
- **Adult Fertility:** not established strongly enough here to assume that adult Obojima Elves reproduce as a stable Elf population
- **Notes:** Treat this as lineage-expression evidence, not proof that every adult Obojima Elf has the normal completed Elf reproductive profile.

# Meaningful Holds retained

## Etherean — Hold

- **Species:** Etherean
- **Source / Version:** `The Griffon's Saddlebag: Book Two`
- **Known Facts:** fixed playable Fey population native to or associated with the Border Ethereal / Ethereal Plane
- **Unresolved Facts:** stable male/female reproductive roles, fertilization method, and live-birth versus egg or other development
- **Status Reason:** being a fixed Fey species and having a society is not enough to invent a reproductive lifecycle
- **Next Resolution Path:** explicit source lore about parents, children, sex differentiation, or birth; otherwise a deliberate homebrew biological decision is required.

## Feathren — Hold

- **Species:** Feathren
- **Source / Version:** `The Griffon's Saddlebag: Book One`
- **Known Facts:** fixed bipedal griffon-descended people combining feline lower-body and avian upper-body traits; source explicitly describes Feathren being born and receiving names at birth
- **Unresolved Facts:** stable male/female reproductive roles and whether development is live-bearing, egg-laying, or another biological route
- **Status Reason:** the species is clearly born, but mixed avian/mammalian anatomy makes choosing a development family without source evidence an unnecessary invention
- **Next Resolution Path:** source-specific reproductive lore or an explicit homebrew decision about which ancestry governs Feathren reproductive anatomy.

---

## Rule consequence

A population name does not automatically deserve a separate reproductive system. Thoric, Varan, and Calidian are biologically Human and therefore inherit Human reproduction.

Conversely, a playable species entry does not automatically imply sexual reproduction. Dara and Geleton are playable species with clearly nonsexual creation/lifecycle mechanisms and therefore remain outside the current breeding pool.
