# Reproductive Species Complete Profiles — Batch 10

## Purpose

This batch resolves several meaningful Holds and unusual biological cases left after the broad family-promotion passes.

It follows:

- `REPRODUCTION_AND_LINEAGE.md`
- `REPRODUCTIVE_FERTILIZATION_COMPATIBILITY.md`
- `REPRODUCTIVE_SPECIES_PROFILES.md`
- `REPRODUCTIVE_SPECIES_PROFILE_INDEX.md`

A specific status in this file overrides an older generic `Candidate` or `Hold` row in `REPRODUCTIVE_SPECIES_CANDIDATE_CATALOG.md` for the same species/version.

## Evidence labels

- **Source-established:** the source directly establishes the reproductive fact.
- **Lore-supported inference:** the source establishes enough anatomy, ancestry, parentage, sex differentiation, birth, or offspring information to support the conclusion.
- **Homebrew biological baseline:** the repository supplies the ordinary reproductive mechanism where the source establishes a stable biological population but does not describe the complete reproductive anatomy.

# Newly completed biological profiles

## Killoren

- **Species:** Killoren
- **Source / Version:** D&D 3.5, `Races of the Wild`
- **Creature Type:** Fey
- **Profile Status:** Complete
- **Evidence Basis:** Source-established male/female sexes, mothers, and born offspring; internal reproductive anatomy is a homebrew biological baseline
- **Compatibility Family:** Internal Live-Bearing Humanoid / Fey Humanoid
- **Reproductive Mode:** Sexual
- **Male Reproductive Role:** supplies the fertilizing biological contribution
- **Female Reproductive Role:** supplies the ovum-equivalent contribution and carries the developing offspring
- **Fertilization Method:** Internal
- **Development Method:** Live-bearing
- **Carrier / Host:** Female parent
- **Special Biological Requirements:** functioning compatible internal-fertilization anatomy
- **Birth / Hatching Constraints:** young are born already manifesting an aspect of nature, commonly matching the aspect their mother manifests at the time; inherited rigid, oversized, winged, or otherwise difficult hybrid traits remain birth-safe under the global delayed-expression rule
- **General Crossbreeding Potential:** Cross-species compatible
- **Known Naturally Compatible:** Killoren + Killoren
- **Known Cross-Species Compatibility:** other completed internal-fertilization biological species when anatomy is workable and no specific incompatibility exists
- **Known Incompatibilities:** magical-only, asexual-only, hermaphroditic-only, shapechanger-dependent, or otherwise incompatible partners under the global gate
- **Notes:** The extraordinary origin of the first Killoren population does not prevent later ordinary biological reproduction because source material explicitly describes Killoren being born, mothers, males, and females.

This profile covers the classic biological sphinx family represented by Androsphinx, Gynosphinx, Criosphinx, and Hieracosphinx in editions where their sexual reproduction is established.

- **Species / Family:** Sphinx
- **Source / Version:** AD&D 2e sphinx ecology; compatible older biological sphinx versions
- **Creature Type:** edition-dependent Magical Beast / Monstrosity equivalent
- **Profile Status:** Complete
- **Evidence Basis:** Source-established male and female forms, mating, and offspring
- **Compatibility Family:** Internal Live-Bearing Mammalian / Sphinx
- **Reproductive Mode:** Sexual
- **Male Reproductive Role:** Androsphinx, Criosphinx, or Hieracosphinx males supply the fertilizing biological contribution
- **Female Reproductive Role:** Gynosphinx supplies the ovum-equivalent contribution and carries the developing offspring
- **Fertilization Method:** Internal
- **Development Method:** Live-bearing under the repository biological baseline, consistent with sphinx young being born in later compatible lore
- **Carrier / Host:** Gynosphinx female
- **Special Biological Requirements:** functioning compatible sphinx reproductive anatomy
- **Birth / Hatching Constraints:** wings, horns, beaks, claws, and other rigid or projecting features remain neonatal-safe and develop progressively after birth when required
- **General Crossbreeding Potential:** Cross-species compatible
- **Known Naturally Compatible:** Sphinx-family pairings supported by the applicable edition's sphinx ecology
- **Known Cross-Species Compatibility:** other completed internal-fertilization species may be evaluated under the normal compatibility engine; no outside pairing is automatic merely because both are internally fertilizing
- **Known Incompatibilities:** species-specific anatomy that cannot support a coherent fertilization or development path
- **Notes:** Classic lore distinguishes one female sphinx form and several male forms. The repository tracks biology separately from behavior, alignment, courtship tradition, or historical coercive lore; all gameplay still follows the adult agency and consent rules.

# Resolved exclusions under the current global gate

## Plasmoid — Not a breeder under current global rules

- **Source / Version:** Spelljammer Plasmoid
- **Status:** Ineligible under current global breeding scope
- **Reason:** Plasmoids lack ordinary internal organs, and older Spelljammer reproductive lore describes genetic exchange followed by splitting rather than a stable biologically male/female sexual reproductive pair.
- **Failed Gate:** required male/female reproductive roles
- **Future Override:** a separate Plasmoid version with established male/female sexual biology may receive its own profile.

## Elan — Not a breeder under current global rules

- **Source / Version:** D&D psionic Elan
- **Status:** Ineligible under current global breeding scope
- **Reason:** Elans are created from existing humans through a transformative psionic process rather than born as the result of ordinary biological sexual reproduction.
- **Failed Gate:** normal biological reproductive lifecycle
- **Future Override:** a distinct stable descendant Elan population that later develops ordinary male/female sexual reproduction may receive a separate profile.

## Kaorti — Not a breeder under current global rules

- **Source / Version:** D&D 3e Kaorti
- **Status:** Ineligible under current global breeding scope
- **Reason:** Kaorti propagate by transforming other creatures into Kaorti; source ecology describes that transformation as effectively their reproductive method.
- **Failed Gate:** ordinary male/female sexual reproduction
- **Future Override:** only a separate stable biological Kaorti variant with established sexual reproduction would qualify.

## Lorwyn Treefolk — Not a breeder under current global rules

- **Source / Version:** Lorwyn / Shadowmoor Treefolk
- **Status:** Ineligible under current global breeding scope
- **Reason:** lore describes male and female Treefolk but indicates both can provide the seed/pollen functions used to propagate their seedbodies, placing their biology outside the repository's currently restricted male/female-role system.
- **Failed Gate:** current exclusion of hermaphroditic / dual-role reproductive biology
- **Future Override:** a campaign may broaden the global reproductive-role rule later.

# Holds retained after research

## Verdan — Hold

Verdan remain a Hold rather than an automatic exclusion. Source material establishes male and female presentations but also describes extensive lifelong physical mutation and changes in gender. It does not establish whether those changes correspond to reproductive-sex changes or how ordinary Verdan conception works.

Do not infer a stable male/female reproductive system until a homebrew decision or stronger source settles that distinction.

## Flamekin — Hold

Current D&D Flamekin are stable Humanoids mechanically adapted from Fire Genasi, but their lore describes bodies fundamentally composed of fire and stone. Published material does not establish a non-magical male/female fertilization, gestation, egg, or birth pathway.

Do not borrow Fire Genasi reproductive anatomy merely because the D&D adaptation borrows Fire Genasi game traits.

## Rimekin — Hold

Rimekin are Flamekin-derived and therefore inherit the unresolved reproductive question rather than automatically inheriting Fire Genasi biology. Once Flamekin biology is established, Rimekin can normally inherit it unless their cold transformation materially changes reproductive anatomy.

## Fairy / generic D&D Fairy — Hold

Fairies are biologically plausible as a fixed male/female Fey people, but current general D&D material does not establish enough reproductive anatomy to choose live birth, eggs, or another biological development route without a deliberate homebrew decision.

## Lorwyn Faerie — Hold

Lorwyn Faeries have a distinct supernatural origin tied to Oona. The source establishes their species and society but does not establish that their continuing population uses ordinary male/female biological sexual reproduction rather than creation or another Fey process.

## Hexblood — Hold by origin/version

Hexblood is a lineage with several mutually different origins. Some Hexbloods are created or transformed; one current origin says a parent sought a child through a bargain with a hag. That proves a Hexblood can exist from a birth-related origin but does not establish one universal Hexblood reproductive system or adult Hexblood fertility.

Profile a specific stable biological Hexblood population separately if a campaign establishes one.

## Dhampir — Hold as an adult breeder; biological parentage bridge established

Current D&D explicitly allows a Dhampir origin in which one parent was a vampire. Therefore at least one D&D Dhampir version provides source evidence that a vampire/mortal biological parentage pathway can exist.

However, that origin does not by itself establish that adult Dhampirs form a stable sexually reproducing species or that every vampire version has functioning reproductive biology.

Use the Dhampir origin as **pairing evidence**, not as proof of universal Dhampir adult fertility, until a version-specific reproductive profile establishes more.

## Research consequence

After this batch, a Hold should remain only because an actual reproductive fact is unresolved. A source's supernatural origin, elemental subtype, Fey type, Outsider type, or unusual adult anatomy is not enough by itself to keep a species on Hold when ordinary male/female birth biology is otherwise established.
