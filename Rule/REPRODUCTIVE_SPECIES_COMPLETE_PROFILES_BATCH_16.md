# Reproductive Species Complete Profiles — Batch 16

## Purpose

This batch cleans up several names that appear in race/species indexes but are biologically Human or Human/Half-Elf populations rather than independent reproductive species. It also records several ancient or obscure beings that remain legitimate Holds because their actual reproductive lifecycle is still unknown.

# Human and Human/Elf population promotions

## Antilan / Sun Mage

- **Species / Population:** Antilan
- **Source / Version:** Spelljammer, `The Astromundi Cluster`
- **Creature Type:** Humanoid (Human)
- **Profile Status:** Complete by family inheritance
- **Inherited Profile:** Human / Internal Live-Bearing Humanoid
- **Evidence Basis:** source explicitly identifies Antilans as a Human race/population native to the Astromundi Cluster
- **Reproductive Mode:** Sexual
- **Fertilization Method:** Internal
- **Development Method:** Live-bearing
- **Carrier / Host:** Female parent
- **General Crossbreeding Potential:** Human-family compatibility plus the normal internal-fertilization engine
- **Notes:** Magical aptitude, unusual eye colors, masks, and culture do not make Antilans reproductively nonhuman.

- **General Crossbreeding Potential:** follows the individual's Human or Human/Elf biological profile

# Meaningful obscure Holds

## Juna — Hold

- **Species:** Juna
- **Source / Version:** ancient Spelljammer precursor race
- **Known Facts:** highly advanced sapient precursor civilization; fixed trilaterally symmetric body with three arms and three legs; worshiped as gods by younger peoples
- **Unresolved Facts:** stable male/female reproductive roles, fertilization method, offspring, eggs/live birth, or another development pathway
- **Status Reason:** technological and god-like advancement provides no reproductive evidence
- **Next Resolution Path:** explicit ecology/lore or deliberate homebrew biological decision.

## Old One / Creator — Hold

- **Species:** Old One / Creator
- **Source / Version:** Forgotten Realms ancient reptilian Creator people
- **Known Facts:** fixed reptilian humanoids, highly intelligent, ancient, magically powerful
- **Unresolved Facts:** stable male/female roles, fertilization, and development method
- **Status Reason:** a reptilian body makes egg-laying plausible but does not prove it; the source lacks enough reproductive evidence for automatic family inheritance
- **Next Resolution Path:** ecology/lore establishing sex differentiation, offspring, eggs, or a deliberate homebrew decision.

## Phaerlock — Hold

- **Species:** Phaerlock
- **Source / Version:** Forgotten Realms `Underdark`
- **Known Facts:** fixed reptilian Humanoid species descended from Lizardfolk that were bred and warped by Phaerimm magic; source recognizes a population rather than one-off constructs
- **Unresolved Facts:** stable male/female roles, whether modern Phaerlocks reproduce among themselves, and whether the founding breeding program remains necessary
- **Status Reason:** being descended from Lizardfolk makes reptilian sexual reproduction plausible, but 'bred from Lizardfolk and powerful magic' does not by itself establish the modern reproductive lifecycle
- **Next Resolution Path:** explicit modern Phaerlock family/egg/sex lore or deliberate stable-lineage homebrew decision.

- **Species:** Lith
- **Source / Version:** Forgotten Realms `Underdark`
- **Known Facts:** sapient/semi-sapient psionic earth-associated magical beasts that speak languages and may appear in pairs

## Catalog consequence

Names found in an old 'race' list must be classified before receiving reproductive anatomy.

A named people can be:

- a true biological species
- a subrace or population of an already completed species
- a stable hybrid lineage
- a culture or character kit usable by more than one species
- a transformed population
- an ancient species with unknown biology

Only the first three normally need independent reproductive biology. Populations and cultures should inherit their actual parent species whenever possible.
