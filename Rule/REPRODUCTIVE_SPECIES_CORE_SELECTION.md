# Reproductive Species Core Selection

## Purpose

This file defines the **curated default species roster** used for ordinary campaign selection and crossbreeding play.

The larger reproductive candidate catalog and completed-profile library remain available for research, expansion, campaign overrides, unusual NPCs, and future additions. This file does **not** delete or invalidate those broader catalogs.

The default roster is intentionally narrowed to:

> **10 major biological families × 10 representative species = 100 core selectable species.**

The goal is to give each family a balanced mix of familiar/plain choices and unusual/high-fantasy choices without overwhelming character creation.

A species listed here is still governed by its completed reproductive profile, family-inheritance profile, version-specific rule, or other applicable reproductive authority. Inclusion here does not make it compatible with every other listed species.

## Selection principles

The core roster favors species that are:

- already Complete or Complete by family inheritance
- biologically understandable under the current male/female sexual-reproduction gate
- useful for varied hybrid combinations
- representative of a major body plan or fantasy lineage
- recognizable enough to be easy to play
- varied enough that the roster does not become ten near-identical Elf branches or ten cosmetic Human variants

Where a species has many biological subraces or cultural branches, one representative core entry normally covers the shared species biology. A campaign can still choose a more specific branch later.

# Core-roster authority

For ordinary campaign setup and species-selection menus:

1. offer the **100 core species in this file** first
2. organize them by the ten families above rather than showing one 100-item flat list
3. allow the player to request **More / Legacy / Obscure / Other Species** when they want something outside the core roster
4. when an outside species is requested, consult `REPRODUCTIVE_SPECIES_PROFILE_INDEX.md` and the larger candidate catalog
5. do not treat absence from this core roster as biological incompatibility or nonexistence
6. campaign-specific rules may add, remove, or replace core selections locally

## Variant handling

Subraces, setting branches, and cultural variants that share the same reproductive biology normally appear beneath the parent species rather than consuming additional core slots.

Examples:

- Elf can branch into High Elf, Wood Elf, Moon Elf, Sea Elf, Astral Elf, Eladrin, Shadar-kai, and setting-specific Elf branches
- Dwarf can branch into setting-specific Dwarf peoples and Duergar when appropriate
- Gnome can branch into Forest, Rock, Deep Gnome, and other established branches
- Halfling can branch into Lightfoot, Strongheart, Ghostwise, Tallfellow, and setting variants
- Orc can branch into Gray Orc and other stable Orc populations
- Dragon species outside the ten iconic True Dragons remain accessible through the extended catalog

This keeps the visible default list manageable while preserving the full homebrew universe underneath it.

## Future maintenance

The 100-species core roster is a **curated default**, not a permanent hard cap.

Replace a core species only when another choice would materially improve variety, recognizability, biological diversity, or campaign usefulness. Do not continuously expand the default menu merely because new species are researched.

The extended reproductive catalog remains unlimited.
