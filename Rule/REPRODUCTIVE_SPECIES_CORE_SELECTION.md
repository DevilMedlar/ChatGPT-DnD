# Reproductive Species Core Selection

## Purpose

This file presents the compact default species roster used during ordinary character creation and crossbreeding selection.

It mirrors `REPRODUCTIVE_SPECIES_CANDIDATE_CATALOG.md`. It is **not** a separate species authority and cannot restore an option removed from the active catalog.

If the two files ever conflict, the active candidate catalog wins and this presentation file must be corrected.

## Global biological gate

Every listed species or family must have:

- stable biological males and females
- physical male/female mating for reproduction
- a workable fertilization pathway
- viable biological offspring development

Campaign-specific rules may add, remove, or replace selections for that campaign.

# Default roster

## Core humanoids

1. **Human**
   - Representative variants: standard Human, Nubari, Umbral Human when heredity is retained
   - Baseline: internal fertilization and live birth

2. **Elf**
   - Representative variants: High Elf, Wood Elf, Sea Elf, Astral Elf, Eladrin
   - Baseline: internal fertilization and live birth

3. **Dwarf**
   - Representative variants: standard Dwarf, Duergar
   - Baseline: internal fertilization and live birth

4. **Gnome**
   - Representative variants: Rock Gnome, Forest Gnome, Deep Gnome / Svirfneblin
   - Baseline: internal fertilization and live birth

## Draconic species

5. **Dragonborn**
   - Representative variants: standard Dragonborn, Floral Dragonborn, Grim Hollow Dragonborn
   - Baseline: internal fertilization and egg-laying

6. **Dragonkin**
   - Representative variants: winged Dragonkin, stable homebrew Dragonkin lineages
   - Baseline: internal fertilization and egg-laying in a fixed draconic body

7. **True Dragon**
   - Representative groups: Chromatic, Metallic, Gem, Deep, Moonstone
   - Baseline: internal fertilization and egg-laying
   - Individual colors and named varieties do not consume separate roster slots unless their reproductive biology materially differs

## Mammalian beastfolk

8. **Feline Beastfolk**
   - Representative variants: Tabaxi, Leonin, Rakasta
   - Baseline: internal fertilization and live birth

9. **Kitsune**
   - Representative variants: natural foxfolk Kitsune, one-tailed and multi-tailed biological lineages
   - Baseline: internal fertilization and live birth through the natural Kitsune body
   - Disguises, illusions, and assumed appearances do not create reproductive anatomy or compatibility

## Reptilian species

10. **Lizardfolk**
    - Representative variant: standard Lizardfolk
    - Baseline: internal fertilization and egg-laying

# Selection rules

- Offer these ten entries as the ordinary global roster.
- Show representative variants beneath the parent species rather than treating them as independent biological species.
- Do not offer removed species through an older profile, deleted batch, historical commit, or remembered chat context.
- A new global option requires the player's explicit approval.
- A campaign-specific option belongs in that campaign's local rules and does not automatically change the global roster.

# Hybrid handling

Hybrid offspring are generated from the two selected parent profiles and the repository's hybrid rules.

A hybrid label does not automatically become a new permanent roster species. It becomes a stable selectable lineage only when the player explicitly establishes it as such and its reproductive biology is defined.
