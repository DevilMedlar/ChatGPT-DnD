# Reproductive Species Complete Profiles — Batch 8

## Purpose

This batch establishes a scalable **family-inheritance promotion rule** for fixed-form biological species whose body plan is plainly modeled on an ordinary sexual animal lineage and whose source does not contradict that biology.

The repository does not need to repeat identical reproductive anatomy for every fox-person, deer-person, bird-person, or dinosaur-person.

A species listed below is `Complete by family inheritance` for its **basic reproductive method**. Pairing-specific compatibility still follows `REPRODUCTIVE_FERTILIZATION_COMPATIBILITY.md`.

## Family-inheritance promotion requirements

A candidate may be promoted by family inheritance only when:

1. it is a fixed biological species rather than a temporary transformation
2. it is not a shapechanger under the current global exclusion
3. its source does not establish asexual-only, magical-only, constructed-only, or otherwise incompatible reproduction
4. its body plan clearly maps to an already completed biological family
5. male/female sexual biology is established or strongly supported by the model
6. no source-specific reproductive fact contradicts the inherited family profile

If later research reveals different biology, the species receives a specific profile that overrides this family inheritance.

### Fixed-form Beastkin

A species called `Beastkin`, `Animalfolk`, or similar is **not automatically Complete merely from the label**.

It may inherit this family when its permanent biological form is a mammalian male/female species and reproduction does not require transformation.

Any version whose defining biology is shapechanging remains outside the current global breeding pool.

## Internal Egg-Laying Arthropod family

- **Profile Status:** Complete family
- **Reproductive Mode:** Sexual
- **Male Reproductive Role:** supplies the fertilizing biological contribution
- **Female Reproductive Role:** supplies ova and lays fertilized eggs
- **Fertilization Method:** Internal biological transfer unless a specific species profile says otherwise
- **Development Method:** Egg-laying
- **Carrier / Host:** Female until laying; eggs develop externally
- **Birth / Hatching Constraints:** chitin, extra limbs, mandibles, adult body size, venom structures, spinnerets, and other rigid traits may mature after hatching
- **General Crossbreeding Potential:** Cross-species compatible with other completed internal-fertilization species only when anatomy can support the required transfer; extreme anatomical differences can make the pairing conditional

# Fishlike family promotions

Fishlike appearance does **not** automatically determine fertilization method because Sahuagin provide a source-established internal-fertilization counterexample.

Therefore there is **no automatic fishlike promotion solely from appearance**.

Species such as Deepborn, Shark-kin, Laneshi, and other aquatic peoples remain specific-profile candidates until internal versus external fertilization is established or deliberately homebrewed.

---

# Family inheritance does not establish every pairing

A family-completed species has enough biology to participate in compatibility checks. It does not mean all cross-family pairings automatically succeed.

Especially check:

- internal transfer anatomy for quadrupeds vs humanoids
- large body-plan differences
- aquatic environmental requirements
- egg incubation requirements
- directional compatibility
- profile-specific incompatibilities

The global carrier-safe development and delayed-trait rules solve **developmental size/shape problems**, but they do not invent a fertilization route where none exists.

---

## Batch-8 outcome

This family inheritance mechanism should be used for future obvious animal-model species rather than creating redundant full profiles one at a time.

Create a bespoke profile only when:

- source lore gives unusual reproduction
- the species crosses family boundaries
- shapechanging or magical origin complicates biology
- fertilization direction matters
- host-dependent development exists
- a known incompatibility exists
- the animal model itself has multiple materially different reproductive methods
