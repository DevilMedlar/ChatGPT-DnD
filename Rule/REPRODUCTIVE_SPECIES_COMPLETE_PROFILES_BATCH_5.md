# Reproductive Species Complete Profiles — Batch 5

## Purpose

This batch completes several Legacy and older-setting biological peoples whose published ecology provides useful reproductive evidence, plus stable homebrew baselines where the body plan clearly fits the repository's biological gate.

It demonstrates an important versioning principle:

> A species name may have more than one reproductive profile when editions or settings materially change how that species comes into existence.

All cross-family interaction uses `REPRODUCTIVE_FERTILIZATION_COMPATIBILITY.md`.

# Saurial

## Saurial — General

- **Species:** Saurial
- **Source / Version:** Forgotten Realms / AD&D 2e and compatible Legacy Saurial versions
- **Creature Type:** Humanoid or source-equivalent reptilian humanoid
- **Profile Status:** Complete
- **Evidence Basis:** Source ecology states that Saurials mate for life, produce one or two eggs each year, and that both male and female parents care for the eggs and hatchlings; internal fertilization is a repository homebrew biological baseline consistent with their reptilian/dinosaurian body plan
- **Compatibility Family:** Internal Egg-Laying Reptilian / Dinosaurian
- **Reproductive Mode:** Sexual
- **Male Reproductive Role:** supplies the fertilizing biological contribution
- **Female Reproductive Role:** supplies ova, receives internal fertilization, forms eggs, and lays them
- **Fertilization Method:** Internal
- **Development Method:** Egg-laying
- **Carrier / Host:** Female until laying; egg then develops externally
- **Special Biological Requirements:** viable egg formation, warmth, protection, and species-appropriate incubation
- **Birth / Hatching Constraints:** hatchlings begin small enough for viable laying and hatching; horns, dorsal plates, tails, wings in Flyer-derived hybrids, and large adult frames can mature after hatching
- **General Crossbreeding Potential:** Cross-species compatible
- **Known Naturally Compatible:** Saurial + Saurial; major Saurial subraces share enough reproductive biology to be naturally compatible under this repository unless a specific campaign says otherwise
- **Known Cross-Species Compatibility:** other completed internal-fertilization species under the global compatibility engine
- **Notes:** Saurial subrace changes phenotype and adult body plan, not the basic egg-laying sexual pathway.

### Saurial subraces inheriting this profile

- Bladeback Saurial
- Finhead / Finback Saurial
- Flyer Saurial
- Hornhead Saurial
- Lacerial / compatible Malatran Saurial branches where the setting retains the same basic biology

# Half-Ogre biological evidence

- **Species:** Half-Ogre / Human-Ogre hybrid lineage
- **Source / Version:** AD&D / Legacy D&D hybrid lineage
- **Creature Type:** Humanoid, Giant-descended, or edition-equivalent
- **Profile Status:** Complete
- **Evidence Basis:** Published D&D material explicitly identifies Half-Ogres as offspring of Human and Ogre parentage
- **Compatibility Family:** Internal Live-Bearing Humanoid / Giant bridge lineage
- **Reproductive Mode:** Sexual
- **Male Reproductive Role:** supplies the fertilizing biological contribution
- **Female Reproductive Role:** supplies the ovum-equivalent contribution and carries the developing offspring
- **Fertilization Method:** Internal
- **Development Method:** Live-bearing
- **Carrier / Host:** Female parent
- **Special Biological Requirements:** ordinary internal-fertilization compatibility under this repository's Human and Ogre profiles
- **Birth / Hatching Constraints:** the universal carrier-safe size rule applies. A Human female does not need to deliver an Ogre-sized newborn; Ogre-derived stature and mass can develop after birth
- **General Crossbreeding Potential:** Cross-species compatible
- **Known Parent Pairing:** Human + Ogre is source-established
- **Directional Rule:** both male Human + female Ogre and male Ogre + female Human are biologically allowed under this repository's homebrew unless a campaign-specific rule says otherwise; carrier-safe prenatal growth removes size alone as an automatic block
- **Later-Generation Fertility:** not yet decided; defer to the future offspring-fertility system rather than importing any edition's sterility assumptions automatically
- **Notes:** This lineage is evidence of Human/Ogre compatibility, not a requirement that all such children use one fixed Half-Ogre stat block.

# Legacy versioning rule reinforced

Batch 5 establishes the following repository precedent:

- **same species name, different reproductive origin = separate profile**
- an older biological version remains usable even if a later edition uses magical transformation or another incompatible origin
- campaign-specific lore can choose which version exists in that campaign
- a campaign can also establish both versions as distinct populations if desired

Examples:

- Legacy Biological Gnoll vs Yeenoghu-Spawn Gnoll
- biological stable lineage vs transformed/constructed version of the same named creature
- a future biological variant of a currently excluded species can receive its own eligible profile without rewriting the excluded version
