# Reproductive Species Complete Profiles

## Purpose and authority

This file owns the reusable reproductive biology for every standard species in the active global whitelist defined by `REPRODUCTIVE_SPECIES_CANDIDATE_CATALOG.md`.

It contains profiles only for:

1. Human
2. Elf
3. Dwarf
4. Gnome
5. Dragonborn
6. Dragonkin
7. True Dragon
8. Kitsune
9. Lizardfolk

`REPRODUCTION_AND_LINEAGE.md` governs compatibility, directional development, carrying-parent rules, adoption, birth-safe hybrid development, fertility, conception, hybrid lineage status, and dice ownership.

A profile establishes biological possibility. It does not create attraction, consent, mating, conception, pregnancy, offspring, or guaranteed adult hybrid fertility.

## Standard-form authority

Every profile in this file applies only to the listed species' **standard canonical form**.

- No subrace, ancestry branch, regional form, setting version, color group, alternate lineage, or third-party variation inherits a profile automatically.
- A nonstandard form requires the player's explicit global approval or an applicable campaign-specific override.
- A culture, class, profession, title, social group, or temporary transformation is not a reproductive species.
- A removed species or variation is not restored merely because its old biology resembles a surviving profile.

## Shared active-roster rules

Every profile in this file uses:

- stable biological males and females
- physical male/female mating
- internal biological fertilization
- either live-bearing or egg-laying development

Unless a profile or campaign-specific rule says otherwise:

- the same standard species is `Naturally compatible`
- two different active-whitelist species are `Cross-species compatible`
- a severe size or anatomy issue can make one reproductive direction `Conditionally compatible` until a real non-magical physical requirement is satisfied
- the female partner determines live pregnancy or egg production
- prenatal or pre-laying size remains within the female partner's viable reproductive range
- difficult inherited structures may develop after birth or hatching

## Shared profile terminology

### Internal Live-Bearing Humanoid

Physical mating transfers the male fertilizing contribution internally. The female supplies the ovum-equivalent contribution, carries the offspring, and gives live birth.

### Internal Live-Bearing Mammalian

The same internal live-bearing structure applies through the standard mammalian body of the species.

### Internal Egg-Laying Draconic

Physical mating transfers the male fertilizing contribution internally. The female supplies the ovum-equivalent contribution, produces and lays a fertilized egg, and the offspring completes development through incubation and hatching.

### Internal Egg-Laying Reptilian

The same internal egg-laying structure applies through reptilian reproductive anatomy and incubation.

# Kitsune

- **Canonical Entry:** Kitsune
- **Form:** Standard natural foxfolk only
- **Creature Type:** Humanoid under the repository homebrew standard
- **Profile Status:** Complete
- **Compatibility Family:** Internal Live-Bearing Mammalian
- **Reproductive Mode:** Sexual, stable male/female
- **Mating Method:** Physical male/female mating through the natural Kitsune body
- **Male Reproductive Role:** supplies the fertilizing biological contribution through internal transfer using natural-body anatomy
- **Female Reproductive Role:** supplies the ovum-equivalent contribution, carries the developing offspring, and gives live birth using natural-body anatomy
- **Fertilization Method:** Internal
- **Development Method:** Live-bearing
- **Carrier:** Female parent
- **Special Requirements:** reproductive compatibility is evaluated from the natural Kitsune body, never from a disguise or assumed appearance
- **Birth Constraints:** ears, fur, tail, claws, fangs, and supernatural markings remain neonatal-safe and may mature gradually
- **Naturally Compatible:** Kitsune + Kitsune
- **Cross-Species Compatible:** every other active-whitelist species when the natural Kitsune body can physically mate through a workable internal-fertilization pathway
- **Natural-Body Exception:** illusions, disguises, humanoid appearances, and assumed forms do not create reproductive anatomy or compatibility; shapechanging is not required for reproduction
- **Notes:** Only the standard Kitsune profile is globally active. This exception applies only to standard Kitsune and does not admit other shapechanger species.

# Nonstandard forms

A nonstandard form does not automatically inherit its standard species profile.

When the player explicitly approves a nonstandard form, define whether it:

- uses the standard profile unchanged
- requires a campaign-specific profile adjustment
- requires a new global profile
- remains outside the reproductive whitelist

Do not infer approval from similarity alone.

# Hybrid profiles

A hybrid does not automatically receive a permanent profile in this file.

Record the individual hybrid's established:

- biological parents
- sex and reproductive anatomy
- live-bearing or egg-laying pathway when relevant
- inherited traits and developmental timing
- adult fertility only after that mechanic is established

Create a permanent stable-lineage profile only after the player explicitly approves that lineage as a recurring selectable biological population.
