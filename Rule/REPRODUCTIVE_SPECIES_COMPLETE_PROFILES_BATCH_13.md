# Reproductive Species Complete Profiles — Batch 13

## Purpose

This batch resolves several important Dark Sun reproductive lineages where the setting provides direct evidence of hybrid birth, sterility, stable descendant breeding, or family reproduction.

The profiles are version-specific. Athasian biology does not automatically override another setting's version of the same broad ancestry.

---

# Mul — Human/Dwarf hybrid bridge, sterile offspring

- **Species / Lineage:** Mul
- **Source / Version:** classic Athasian / Dark Sun Mul
- **Creature Type:** Humanoid hybrid
- **Profile Status:** Complete as hybrid-development evidence; Not a breeder as an adult lineage
- **Evidence Basis:** Source-established Human/Dwarf crossbreeding and source-established Mul sterility
- **Parent Pair:** Human + Dwarf
- **Reproductive Mode of Parents:** Sexual
- **Fertilization Method:** Internal
- **Development Method:** Live-bearing
- **Normal Carrier:** Human female in the classic Athasian breeding tradition
- **Birth / Development Viability:** Viable hybrid offspring are source-established
- **Adult Offspring Fertility:** Sterile in the classic Athasian version
- **General Crossbreeding Consequence:** Human and Dwarf are source-established cross-species compatible for producing a Mul on Athas
- **Known Incompatibility of Mul Adult:** Mul cannot naturally perpetuate the Mul lineage in the classic Athasian version
- **Notes:** This profile is a major example of why parent-pair compatibility, birth viability, and adult offspring fertility are separate mechanics. A pairing can successfully produce a child even when that child is sterile.

## Birth-safety homebrew adjustment

Some classic descriptions portray Mul pregnancy as exceptionally dangerous because of the offspring's robust frame. Under this repository's global birth-safe development rule, a campaign does **not** need to reproduce unavoidable lethal childbirth merely to preserve the Mul lineage.

The developing Mul remains within the carrying parent's viable prenatal and delivery range, with exceptional adult bulk, bone structure, and musculature developing progressively after birth unless a campaign explicitly opts into harsher source-specific obstetric consequences.

# First-Generation Dray

- **Species:** First-Generation Dray
- **Source / Version:** AD&D 2e `City by the Silt Sea`
- **Creature Type:** draconic humanoid, edition-dependent
- **Profile Status:** Complete
- **Evidence Basis:** Source explicitly states that the first successful but mutated Dray did not die out and were capable of breeding; their modern population consists of descendants
- **Compatibility Family:** Internal Egg-Laying Draconic / Stable Transformed Lineage
- **Reproductive Mode:** Sexual
- **Male Reproductive Role:** supplies the fertilizing biological contribution
- **Female Reproductive Role:** supplies the ovum-equivalent contribution, forms the fertilized egg, and lays it under the repository draconic-family baseline
- **Fertilization Method:** Internal
- **Development Method:** Egg-laying, homebrew family baseline consistent with the stable reptilian/draconic body plan
- **Carrier / Host:** Female parent until laying; egg develops through incubation afterward
- **Special Biological Requirements:** functioning compatible internal-fertilization anatomy and viable incubation
- **Birth / Hatching Constraints:** mutations and difficult draconic structures remain within viable egg/hatchling limits; severe adult protrusions or heavy structures develop after hatching when necessary
- **General Crossbreeding Potential:** Cross-species compatible
- **Known Naturally Compatible:** First-Generation Dray + First-Generation Dray
- **Known Cross-Species Compatibility:** other completed internal-fertilization species may be evaluated normally under the global engine
- **Notes:** Their founding transformation was magical, but their descendants are a stable biological population. Each new Dray does not require the original transformation process.

- **Evidence Basis:** Source-established two-parent families and repeated statement that Pterrans bear young in pairs; exact fertilization/delivery anatomy uses the repository reptilian-family baseline

- **Reproductive Mode:** Sexual
- **Male Reproductive Role:** supplies the fertilizing biological contribution

- **Development Method:** Egg-laying

- **Typical Offspring Pattern:** paired offspring / two young per reproductive event under source-established family ecology
- **Special Biological Requirements:** functioning compatible internal-fertilization anatomy and viable incubation conditions
- **Birth / Hatching Constraints:** paired eggs remain within the female parent's viable laying range; crests, beaks, claws, or other rigid features remain hatch-safe and develop further after hatching

## Dark Sun versioning consequence

Dark Sun repeatedly demonstrates that a magical or engineered origin can lead to several different reproductive outcomes:

- **Half-Giant:** magically founded, later fertile with its own stable lineage
- **Dray:** magically transformed founders, later stable breeding descendants
- **Mul:** naturally produced hybrid child, but sterile adult

Therefore origin and fertility must remain separate fields. Never infer adult fertility solely from the fact that a hybrid can be born, and never infer magical reproduction solely from the fact that the founding population was created through magic.
