# Reproductive Species Complete Profiles — Batch 3

## Purpose

This batch completes several high-value unusual biological groups:

- true giants and related giant humanoids
- Yuan-ti major breeds
- Githyanki
- named true-dragon families inheriting the general True Dragon profile

All cross-family interaction uses `REPRODUCTIVE_FERTILIZATION_COMPATIBILITY.md`.

# Internal Live-Bearing Giant family

## Shared giant biological profile

- **Profile Status:** Complete
- **Evidence Basis:** D&D consistently presents true giants as humanlike biological peoples with male and female individuals; exact reproductive mechanics are a repository homebrew biological baseline
- **Reproductive Mode:** Sexual
- **Male Reproductive Role:** supplies the fertilizing biological contribution
- **Female Reproductive Role:** supplies the ovum-equivalent contribution and carries the developing offspring
- **Fertilization Method:** Internal
- **Development Method:** Live-bearing
- **Carrier / Host:** Female parent
- **Special Biological Requirements:** functioning compatible internal-fertilization anatomy
- **Birth Constraints:** offspring remains within the female carrier's viable prenatal and delivery range; giant adult size develops progressively after birth
- **General Crossbreeding Potential:** Cross-species compatible
- **Cross-Family Rule:** other completed internal-fertilization species may crossbreed when no specific incompatibility exists; a non-giant female does not gestate a giant-sized newborn

## Giant crossbreeding examples

- male Goliath + female Storm Giant -> female Giant carries and gives live birth; offspring can grow toward a giant-derived adult frame later
- male Frost Giant + female Human -> female Human carries a Human-safe newborn; giant stature and mass can emerge through later growth
- male Dragon + female Cloud Giant -> female Giant carries and gives live birth because the female is live-bearing
- male Fire Giant + female Dragon -> female Dragon forms and lays the hybrid egg because the female is egg-laying

These examples establish development method only. Hybrid phenotype and power inheritance remain separate.

# Yuan-ti major breeds

## Yuan-ti — General

- **Species:** Yuan-ti
- **Source / Version:** Cross-edition Forgotten Realms / current Yuan-ti lineage where biologically applicable
- **Creature Type:** Humanoid for current player-facing Yuan-ti; older forms vary by edition
- **Profile Status:** Complete
- **Evidence Basis:** Older D&D/Realms ecology explicitly describes Yuan-ti breeding, female clutches, brood chambers, eggs, and hatchlings
- **Compatibility Family:** Internal Egg-Laying Serpentine
- **Reproductive Mode:** Sexual
- **Male Reproductive Role:** supplies the fertilizing biological contribution
- **Female Reproductive Role:** supplies ova, forms fertilized eggs, and lays the clutch
- **Fertilization Method:** Internal
- **Development Method:** Egg-laying
- **Carrier / Host:** Female parent until laying; brood chamber/incubation after laying
- **Special Biological Requirements:** viable egg formation and warm/species-appropriate incubation conditions
- **Birth / Hatching Constraints:** hatchlings begin small and comparatively flexible; large serpentine bodies, hoods, heavy scales, venom apparatus, and unusual limb configurations can develop further after hatching
- **General Crossbreeding Potential:** Cross-species compatible
- **Known Naturally Compatible:** same Yuan-ti breed; source lore also supports interbreeding among major Yuan-ti breeds
- **Known Cross-Species Compatibility:** other completed internal-fertilization species under the global compatibility engine
- **Known Conditional Pairings:** external-fertilization species require an explicit biological bridge
- **Notes:** Yuan-ti origin involved ancient magical experimentation, but established adult Yuan-ti reproduction is biological sexual reproduction and therefore qualifies under the current global gate.

### Yuan-ti Pureblood

- **Profile Status:** Complete
- **Inherits:** Yuan-ti — General
- **Development:** Egg-laying under the cross-edition Yuan-ti biological profile
- **Crossbreeding:** Cross-species compatible with completed internal-fertilization species
- **Birth-safe note:** when a male Pureblood breeds with a live-bearing female species, the female's live-bearing biology governs development instead of requiring an egg

### Yuan-ti Malison / Halfblood

- **Profile Status:** Complete
- **Inherits:** Yuan-ti — General
- **Development:** Egg-laying
- **Crossbreeding:** Cross-species compatible with completed internal-fertilization species
- **Birth-safe note:** serpentine tails, snake heads, extra serpentine structures, or similar inherited features can develop after birth when the female carrier is a live-bearing non-Yuan-ti species

### Yuan-ti Abomination

- **Profile Status:** Complete
- **Inherits:** Yuan-ti — General
- **Development:** Egg-laying
- **Crossbreeding:** Cross-species compatible with completed internal-fertilization species when the individual anatomy can physically support the required reproductive pathway
- **Special note:** extreme body-form differences can make a specific pairing `Conditionally compatible` even though the species uses internal fertilization

### Yuan-ti Anathema and unusual advanced forms

- **Profile Status:** Partial / profile individually
- **Reason:** extraordinary transformations or advanced forms may alter anatomy enough that the general Yuan-ti profile cannot be assumed without checking the specific version

# Githyanki

## Githyanki

- **Species:** Githyanki
- **Source / Version:** D&D multiverse / current and legacy Githyanki lore
- **Creature Type:** Humanoid
- **Profile Status:** Complete
- **Evidence Basis:** D&D Beyond lore explicitly states that Githyanki lay eggs
- **Compatibility Family:** Internal Egg-Laying Gith
- **Reproductive Mode:** Sexual
- **Male Reproductive Role:** supplies the fertilizing biological contribution
- **Female Reproductive Role:** supplies ova, forms fertilized eggs, and lays them
- **Fertilization Method:** Internal
- **Development Method:** Egg-laying
- **Carrier / Host:** Female parent until laying; egg then develops in a suitable crèche/incubation environment
- **Special Biological Requirements:** a developmental environment in which normal biological growth and hatching can proceed; Astral timelessness may require a non-Astral crèche depending on the campaign's planar rules
- **Birth / Hatching Constraints:** hatchlings remain small enough for viable laying and hatching; adult psionic and physical development follows later growth
- **General Crossbreeding Potential:** Cross-species compatible
- **Known Naturally Compatible:** Githyanki + Githyanki
- **Known Cross-Species Compatibility:** other completed internal-fertilization species when reproductive anatomy and development are viable
- **Known Conditional Pairings:** any pairing whose egg must develop in an environment incompatible with the other ancestry may require a viable crèche condition
- **Notes:** Githzerai do not automatically inherit this egg-laying profile merely because both peoples descend from ancient Gith. Their reproduction remains separately profiled.

# Tanarukk

## Tanarukk

- **Species:** Tanarukk
- **Source / Version:** Forgotten Realms / older D&D fiend-touched Orc lineage
- **Creature Type:** varies by edition; commonly fiendish/orc-related monstrous humanoid
- **Profile Status:** Complete for the stable biological lineage version
- **Evidence Basis:** source lore establishes Tanarukks as Orcs with fiendish ancestry; reproductive details use the stable Orc-lineage homebrew baseline
- **Compatibility Family:** Internal Live-Bearing Humanoid / Orc-derived
- **Reproductive Mode:** Sexual
- **Male Reproductive Role:** supplies the fertilizing biological contribution
- **Female Reproductive Role:** supplies the ovum-equivalent contribution and carries the developing offspring
- **Fertilization Method:** Internal
- **Development Method:** Live-bearing
- **Carrier / Host:** Female parent
- **Special Biological Requirements:** functioning compatible internal-fertilization anatomy
- **Birth / Hatching Constraints:** horns, heavy musculature, claws, unusually thick skin, or fiendish traits may develop progressively after birth
- **General Crossbreeding Potential:** Cross-species compatible
- **Known Naturally Compatible:** Tanarukk + Tanarukk where the setting treats them as a stable breeding lineage
- **Known Cross-Species Compatibility:** Orc and other completed internal-fertilization species under the global compatibility engine
- **Known Incompatibilities:** magical-only fiendish propagation does not replace this biological profile
- **Notes:** The existence of fiendish ancestry does not make reproduction magical when the adult Tanarukk itself belongs to a stable biological lineage.

The following remain deliberately unresolved rather than guessed:

- Githzerai developmental method
- Fey'ri versions with inherent shapechanging
- Succubus/Incubus under the current no-shapechanger global rule
- Cambion versions whose adult reproductive biology and shapechanging status are not yet fixed for this repository
- Half-Giant versions from settings where infertility is part of the source premise
- Troll and Trollkin versions where regeneration, mutation, or setting-specific fertility needs separate handling
- Sphinx reproductive biology
- unusual divine or fiend species whose offspring are source-established but whose own stable male/female reproduction is not

These Holds can become Complete when a coherent version-specific biological profile is established.
