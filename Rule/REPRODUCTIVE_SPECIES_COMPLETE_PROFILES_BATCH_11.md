# Reproductive Species Complete Profiles — Batch 11

## Purpose

This batch promotes additional fixed biological peoples from older D&D monster and setting material by using already completed reproductive families.

These promotions use the family-inheritance rule in `REPRODUCTIVE_SPECIES_PROFILE_INDEX.md`.

A species is promoted only when its established form is biologically stable, its source does not establish a conflicting reproductive process, and its body plan maps cleanly to an existing family.

Pairing-specific compatibility still follows `REPRODUCTIVE_FERTILIZATION_COMPATIBILITY.md`.

# Goblinoid and gnoll-family promotions

## Forestkith Goblin

- **Source / Version:** D&D 3.5, `Monster Manual III`
- **Profile Status:** Complete by family inheritance
- **Inherited Profile:** Goblin / Internal Live-Bearing Humanoid
- **Evidence Basis:** Source identifies Forestkith Goblins as Humanoid (Goblinoid), includes males and females, and describes stable packs and societies
- **Reproductive Mode:** Sexual
- **Fertilization Method:** Internal
- **Development Method:** Live-bearing
- **Carrier / Host:** Female parent
- **General Crossbreeding Potential:** Cross-species compatible under the Goblin family rules
- **Notes:** Tree Shape is a supernatural camouflage ability and is not used as a reproductive form. Reproduction follows the fixed Goblin body rather than the temporary tree state.

## Flind

- **Source / Version:** D&D 3.5 biological Gnoll / Flind lineage
- **Profile Status:** Complete by family inheritance
- **Inherited Profile:** Legacy biological Gnoll / Internal Live-Bearing Mammalian-Humanoid
- **Evidence Basis:** Flinds are a Gnoll lineage in the applicable source tradition
- **Reproductive Mode:** Sexual
- **Fertilization Method:** Internal
- **Development Method:** Live-bearing
- **Carrier / Host:** Female parent
- **General Crossbreeding Potential:** Cross-species compatible under the biological Gnoll family rules
- **Notes:** This profile does not apply to a source version that makes the individual a magically spawned or otherwise non-biological creature.

# Mammalian and batfolk promotions

## Goatfolk / Ibixian

- **Source / Version:** D&D 3.5, `Monster Manual III`
- **Creature Type:** Monstrous Humanoid
- **Profile Status:** Complete by family inheritance
- **Inherited Profile:** Internal Live-Bearing Mammalian / Beastfolk
- **Evidence Basis:** Source describes a fixed goat/human biological body plan and distinct males and females
- **Reproductive Mode:** Sexual
- **Fertilization Method:** Internal
- **Development Method:** Live-bearing
- **Carrier / Host:** Female parent
- **Birth / Hatching Constraints:** horns begin sufficiently small or developmentally immature for viable live birth and grow afterward
- **General Crossbreeding Potential:** Cross-species compatible with other completed internal-fertilization species when anatomy is workable
- **Notes:** Their resemblance to Satyrs does not make them Fey and does not change the basic mammalian reproductive model.

# Lizardfolk-family promotions

## Blackscale Lizardfolk

- **Source / Version:** D&D 3.5, `Monster Manual III`
- **Profile Status:** Complete by family inheritance
- **Inherited Profile:** Lizardfolk / Internal Egg-Laying Reptilian
- **Reproductive Mode:** Sexual
- **Fertilization Method:** Internal
- **Development Method:** Egg-laying
- **Carrier / Host:** Female parent until laying; egg develops through incubation afterward
- **Birth / Hatching Constraints:** hatchling and shell size follow the female parent's viable laying range
- **General Crossbreeding Potential:** Cross-species compatible under the reptilian internal-fertilization rules
- **Notes:** Large adult body size is expressed through later growth rather than requiring proportionally oversized eggs.

## Poison Dusk Lizardfolk

- **Source / Version:** D&D 3.5, `Monster Manual III`
- **Profile Status:** Complete by family inheritance
- **Inherited Profile:** Lizardfolk / Internal Egg-Laying Reptilian
- **Reproductive Mode:** Sexual
- **Fertilization Method:** Internal
- **Development Method:** Egg-laying
- **Carrier / Host:** Female parent until laying; egg develops through incubation afterward
- **Birth / Hatching Constraints:** offspring remain within the female parent's viable egg size; larger or unusual hybrid features develop after hatching
- **General Crossbreeding Potential:** Cross-species compatible under the reptilian internal-fertilization rules
- **Notes:** Small adult stature is handled through the same carrier-safe size rule as other species.

# Family promotion rule reinforced

After this batch, a source need not describe conception explicitly for every obvious branch of an already completed species family.

For example:

- a Goblin branch remains Goblin biology unless the source says otherwise
- a Gnoll branch remains the applicable biological Gnoll biology unless the source says otherwise
- a Lizardfolk branch remains Lizardfolk biology unless the source says otherwise
- a fixed mammalian animalfolk species can use the mammalian family baseline
- a stable living Giant people can use the Giant baseline

Do **not** use this rule where the source establishes transformation-only creation, asexual reproduction, hermaphroditic reproduction, undead creation, construct manufacture, magical-only manifestation, or another conflicting lifecycle.
