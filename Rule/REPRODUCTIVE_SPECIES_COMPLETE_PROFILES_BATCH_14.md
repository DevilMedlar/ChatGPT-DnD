# Reproductive Species Complete Profiles — Batch 14

## Purpose

This batch resolves fixed biological peoples from `Oriental Adventures` and related eastern D&D material, while preserving the current global exclusion for species whose reproductive identity depends on full shapechanging.

# Spirit Folk family

This profile covers stable Bamboo, River, Sea, and compatible Forgotten Realms Spirit Folk populations where adults exist as fixed humanlike male/female humanoids rather than requiring each new individual to be created directly by a nature spirit.

- **Species / Family:** Spirit Folk
- **Source / Version:** `Oriental Adventures`, Kara-Tur, and compatible `Unapproachable East` Spirit Folk
- **Creature Type:** Humanoid with spirit ancestry
- **Profile Status:** Complete as stable descendant lineage
- **Evidence Basis:** Source establishes Spirit Folk as descendants of Humans and nature spirits, presents them as stable playable peoples, and compatible Forgotten Realms material explicitly recognizes male and female Spirit Folk
- **Compatibility Family:** Internal Live-Bearing Humanoid / Spirit Lineage
- **Reproductive Mode:** Sexual
- **Male Reproductive Role:** supplies the fertilizing biological contribution
- **Female Reproductive Role:** supplies the ovum-equivalent contribution and carries the developing offspring
- **Fertilization Method:** Internal
- **Development Method:** Live-bearing
- **Carrier / Host:** Female parent
- **Special Biological Requirements:** functioning compatible internal-fertilization anatomy; the lineage's bond to river, sea, bamboo, or another natural feature is inherited separately from conception
- **Birth / Hatching Constraints:** ordinary humanlike live-birth constraints
- **General Crossbreeding Potential:** Cross-species compatible
- **Known Naturally Compatible:** Spirit Folk + compatible Spirit Folk populations
- **Known Cross-Species Compatibility:** Humans are ancestral-source compatible; other completed internal-fertilization species may be evaluated normally
- **Notes:** Some historical Spirit Folk origin stories involve nature spirits or dragons assuming human form to produce children with Humans. Those founding pairings can violate the current no-shapechanger reproductive gate if the transformation is required for conception. The stable adult Spirit Folk population is profiled separately because it exists in a fixed humanlike body and does not need to repeat the founding transformation for every descendant under this homebrew baseline.

## River Spirit Folk ancestry note

Older Kara-Tur material explicitly establishes Human offspring with Chiang Lung and Shen Lung river dragons, producing River Spirit Folk. This is valuable ancestry and hybrid evidence, but because those dragon/human conceptions involve dragons assuming human form, the original pairing is **not** automatically available under the current global no-shapechanger rule.

A campaign override can re-enable that source-specific parent bridge without changing the stable Spirit Folk profile.

# Resolved exclusions

## Hengeyokai — Not a breeder under current global rules

- **Source / Version:** `Oriental Adventures`
- **Status:** Ineligible under current global breeding scope
- **Reason:** Hengeyokai are explicitly shapechanging animals that freely alternate among animal, humanoid, and hybrid forms. Their reproductive lore also includes mating in different forms in some versions.
- **Failed Gate:** current global exclusion of shapechanger species and reproduction dependent on alternate forms
- **Future Override:** a campaign may explicitly allow Hengeyokai reproduction later.

## Anakore — Not a breeder under current global rules

- **Source / Version:** Dark Sun / Dragon #387 Anakore
- **Status:** Ineligible under current global breeding scope
- **Reason:** Source states no discernible characteristics distinguish Anakores as male or female and rumors that they produce no young; selected captives instead transform into new Anakores inside subterranean womb-like chambers.
- **Failed Gate:** no stable male/female reproductive roles and no ordinary biological sexual reproduction
- **Future Override:** a different Anakore version with established sexual male/female biology may receive a separate profile.

# Retained Hold

## Gloura — Hold

Glouras remain a Hold. Published material establishes a fixed Fey humanoid with mothlike wings, stable intelligence, and a biological-looking body, but does not establish male/female reproductive roles, offspring, conception, eggs, or live birth strongly enough to choose a reproductive family without a deliberate homebrew ruling.

Because the purpose of a Hold is now to represent a **real missing biological fact**, Gloura is an appropriate Hold rather than an automatic exclusion.
