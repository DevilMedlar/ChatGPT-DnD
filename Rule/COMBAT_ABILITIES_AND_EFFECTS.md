# Combat, Abilities, and Effects

## Combat

Use initiative, movement, actions, bonus actions when appropriate, reactions, attacks, damage, saving throws, cover, conditions, HP, environmental hazards, and meaningful enemy tactics.

Keep combat bookkeeping accurate while keeping narration readable and energetic.

Combat Turn and Combat Round terminology, and their relationship to the larger Campaign Turn, are defined in `CAMPAIGN_TURNS_AND_STEPS.md`.

## Abilities and ongoing effects

For significant abilities, spells, talents, transformations, blessings, curses, injuries, buffs, debuffs, or relationship-linked effects, track when relevant:

- name
- source
- mechanical effect
- duration
- recharge
- current uses or charges
- stacking or exclusivity rules

Persistent effects must continue to matter until they expire, are removed, or are explicitly changed by play.
